DUELL

MultiMorphing
Bitimbral Synthesizer
by OSCILL/R

Two synths in one small box. DUELL is free, open-source firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. It turns the FM-1 into a bitimbral instrument: two complete parts, three ways to play them together (SPLIT, STEREO, LAYER), and a morph that glides between two versions of every sound.

Open the emulator

Public beta: the DUELL 0.2.4 beta firmware for the FM-1 and the browser emulator are available now.

The firmware has been run on one real FM-1 by its author. The way back to the official firmware has worked about three times, on the author's unit, and has not been tried on others. Installing is at your own risk: read the install guide and the recovery notes first.

  • Open the emulatorDUELL in your browser, no FM-1 needed. Click the page once to start the sound. It keeps your saved data in the browser.
  • Installer (beta)Flash DUELL to an FM-1, or go back to the official firmware. At your own risk.
  • Quick startThe short tour. More guides are in the docs folder: install, recovery, troubleshooting, known issues, testing.
  • Report a bugFound something in the emulator? Please open an issue with the bug report form.
  • ReleasesThe firmware package, the emulator build and the release notes.
  • Source code on GitHubThe full design (SPEC.md) is there too.
