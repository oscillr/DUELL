DUELL

MultiMorphing
Bitimbral Synthesizer
by OSCILL/R

Two synths in one small box. DUELL is free, open-source firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. It turns the FM-1 into a bitimbral instrument: two complete parts, three ways to play them together (LAYER, DUEL, STEREO), and a morph that glides between two versions of every sound.

Web emulator: coming soon

DUELL 1.0: the firmware for the FM-1 and the web editor are available now. Web emulator: coming soon.

The firmware has been run on one real FM-1 by its author. The way back to the official firmware has worked about three times, on the author's unit, and has not been tried on others. Installing is at your own risk: read the install guide and the recovery notes first.
