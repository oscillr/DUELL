# Changelog

**DUELL**
MultiMorphing Bitimbral Synthesizer
by OSCILL/R

Each release: a one-line summary, then New, Fixed, Compatibility, Install, Files.

## DUELL 0.2.13 beta

### Changed
- **New order of the factory multis.** M01 REESE SWAMP, M02 WHISPER TIDE, M03 EERIE SIGNAL, M04 WIRED SNAIL, M05 DEAD BUFFER, M06 ACID ETHER, M07 COLD CHAPEL, M08 GARGLE ACID, M09 NIGHT ORGAN, M10 FINAL REEL. M11 and M12 stay empty. MIDI program changes follow the new numbers (program 0 = M01).
  - **A new unit starts with M01 REESE SWAMP**, in LAYER with its BALANCE (also after MENU > SYSTEM > ERASE DATA).
  - Your own saved multis stay in their slots. If one of yours sits in a slot that now has a different factory multi, yours wins there; ERASE of yours brings that slot's factory multi back.
- **M01 REESE SWAMP: MORPH goes further.** Its AMOUNT is 17 % on both parts (was 7 %), and End B is new: moving the MORPH fader to B now clearly changes the sound (brighter and grittier, about the same loudness). Before, End B barely changed anything.
- **The six newer factory multis (now M01, M02, M07..M10) are driven by the keys: press a key or a chord and both parts play, no PLAY needed.** Nothing plays on its own any more: the stored step patterns are gone, the grooves are arps on the keys you hold (they start with the key, on the internal clock), and a different key moves both parts with it.
  - **M07 COLD CHAPEL** (slow): the pad latches the chord (HOLD ALL); the singing bowl plays the chord's notes one by one, upwards, a half note each, and keeps going after you let go. The next chord replaces it. MORPH towards End B opens it up. The bowl now plays one note at a time, which brings the CPU down (with the MORPH fader in the middle about 70 % instead of 96 %).
  - **M02 WHISPER TIDE** (slow): the pad latches the chord; the whisper choir picks a note of it at random, one per bar, over two octaves, and keeps going after you let go.
  - **M08 GARGLE ACID** (fast, 127 BPM): the growl bass plays the key you hold, two octaves down; the acid sound plays a 16-step riff in 1/16 notes on that key, an octave above the bass, with rests, octave jumps and accents. Let go and both stop.
  - **M01 REESE SWAMP** (fast, 124 BPM): the Reese bass plays the key, two octaves down, wobbling at 1/4; the acid sound loops an 8-step 1/16 riff on the key, two octaves above it. It no longer stores a key split.
  - **M09 NIGHT ORGAN** (calmer, 121 BPM): one key gives a minor-seventh organ chord, stabbed on the off-beat of beats 1 and 3 (a half-time feel, 1/8 notes, long stabs); the bass plays the key on the off-beats in 1/8 notes, the last an octave up.
  - **M10 FINAL REEL** (slow): the brass and the strings both latch the chord you play (HOLD ALL), the strings an octave higher; no arp.
- **PLAY no longer plays a part's step pattern by itself when that part's arp is set to SEQUENCER.** The pattern belongs to the arp then: it plays on the keys you hold, as the riff of the key. Before, PLAY also played the same steps from C4, so a SEQUENCER arp with PLAY pressed played two things at once.

## DUELL 0.2.11 beta

### Added
- **QUICK GLO: hold GLO.** MUTE 1, MUTE 2, LEVEL 1, LEVEL 2 and the level meters over the page you are on (PERFORM too); the keys play; let go and you are back. A tap of GLO opens the GLO page as before.
- **A short guide at the first start.** After the logo, a new unit shows six pages: the three modes; MORPH; the long presses (FX for PERFORM; EDIT, LFO, ENV, ARP or GLO for quick edit); REC as HOLD; resetting a knob with OCT-; STOP ALL and SAVE OVER. On that start the logo stays about half a second longer.
- **It also shows once after updating from an older build.** After that it does not come back by itself.
- **SELECT or OCT+ turns to the next page, SELECT left or OCT- goes back.** HOME closes the guide. EDIT, FX, ENV, LFO, ARP, SCL, GLO, SEQ and SAVE close it and go straight to their own page or job.
- **You can play while it is shown.** The keys, MIDI, PLAY/STOP and REC (HOLD) work as usual. The knobs do nothing until the guide is closed (the top line says HOME closes it).
- **MENU > SYSTEM > GUIDE shows the guide again** at any time.
- **Six new factory multis, M05..M10, all in LAYER.** Two ambient ones to play with HOLD ALL (the keys latch): M05 COLD CHAPEL (#51 EVOLVING PAD + #46 SINGING BOWL) and M06 WHISPER TIDE (#50 DIGITAL PAD + #42 WHISPER PAD); MORPH towards End B opens them up. Two bass ones with stored patterns, press PLAY: M07 GARGLE ACID (#15 GROWL BASS + #4 SQUELCH) and M08 REESE SWAMP (#17 REESE BASS + #4 SQUELCH; its keys split at C4 when MODE RECALL is ON, below). A house one: M09 NIGHT ORGAN (#79 DRAWBARS minor-seventh stabs on the off-beats + #2 LADDER BASS). A slow cinematic one: M10 FINAL REEL (#48 BRASS PAD + #44 STRING MACH). Numbers are PATCHES numbers. If you saved your own multi into M05..M10, yours stays; ERASE of it brings the factory multi back.

### Changed
- **Loading a multi no longer changes the output mode, BALANCE, XFADE or SPLIT.** The unit stays as set when a multi is loaded, also by MIDI program change. MENU > CONTROL > MODE RECALL (default OFF) set to ON brings back the old behaviour: a load applies the mode, BALANCE, XFADE and SPLIT saved in the multi. Saving a multi still stores them. A new unit's first start still loads all of M01.
- **Key lights.** The root key of the scale is lit at full power and never pulses. The other keys of the scale are brighter (MEDIUM: half of full). Keys that are played or sounding (held, arp, sequencer, chords) pulse slowly between full and a bright trough, following the tempo's beat while the sequencer or an arp runs. In PERFORM the first key of each row of four glows steadily so the rows can be counted; the other effect keys are dark until held.
- **MORPH words.** SMART has two states, shown as SMART (on) or WILD, never "SMART OFF". The pair is written A<>B on the ROLL and UNDO cards. BOTH is just BOTH ("BOTH", "PART 1", "PART 2" in the header message and on the MORPH SET panel). ARP and PITCH say ARP ON / ARP OFF and PITCH ON / PITCH OFF.
- **SMART MORPH switches things on less often, so the CPU stays lower with the fader moved.** A roll in SMART now turns on something that is off in the sound only half as often as before: the multimode filter when it is off, DIST, the chorus, delay and reverb sends, SPREAD, and the filter and LFO depths that are at 0. When it does, it works exactly as before; WILD is unchanged. The factory multis with the fader in the middle use up to about 17 % less CPU (M02 92 % to 75 %, M14 94 % to 87 %, M15 114 % to 107 %, measured on the computer model). Because the random End B of every sound is made again from its saved seed, an End B saved with 0.2.10 or earlier can sound a little different now. The End B of 12 factory sounds got a new seed so each still keeps its loudness: #10 BULLFROG, #24 PIXEL HERO, #33 PURPLE FLUTE, #43 CHIP CHAPEL, #51 EVOLVING PAD, #61 DOO WOP, #64 GHOST HARP, #67 DIGI PLUCK, #76 FAUX HARPSI, #91 CRYSTAL CAVE, #96 LESLIE STORM and #97 RAIN CHIMES.
- **Fewer voices for very heavy combinations.** When the two parts' sounds together would need more than the processor has (for example a heavy WAVE pad with the 24 dB filter next to a light sound), the heavier part now plays at most as many voices as fit, never fewer than 4. Notes that are already sounding are never cut; only new notes are limited. Most sounds and all factory multis keep their full voices.

### Fixed
- **Holding EDIT (QUICK FILTER): the keys play.** The first white key (F3) used to start INITIALIZE SOUND? and the black keys did DRUM lane mutes, so playing an F could erase the sound. All keys now play in every quick layer. INIT stays on EDIT > ENGINE, K4. The DRUM lane mutes that were on the black keys are gone from there.
- **The header in a quick layer shows the one part it edits,** also with ALGORITHM at BOTH on PERFORM or MORPH.
- **REC as HOLD in LAYER holds both parts.** The keys play both parts in LAYER, but REC switched HOLD on the selected part only, so the other part's chord or arpeggio stopped when you let go. REC now switches every part the keys play, each by its own arp state, and the REC light is bright when any of them holds.
- **Loading a multi resets SPACE.** Since 0.2.10 SPACE keeps its value across PERFORM, so a value set earlier was still there after loading another multi (PERFORM showed 66). Every multi load (SAVE > MULTIS, program change, the editor, power-on) now sets SPACE and the PERFORM FILTER to 0; it glides, so no click. Loading a sound (PRESETS) does not touch it.
- **No click the first time a QUICK layer is opened while playing.** The small one-time "layer seen" setting was written to flash at once, and the write stops the audio for a moment. Settings are now only written when nothing sounds: no key or button held, no voice (release tails included), transport stopped.
- **Saving no longer clicks.** While a patch, a multi or an editor backup is written to flash, the sound used to stop dead for about 40 ms and start again with a click. Now the output fades down over 3 ms before the flash write and fades back in after it, a short soft dip. Notes you hold keep sounding; nothing waits and the SAVE dialogs are unchanged. The TEST screen (developer builds) shows the longest flash erase measured since power-on.
- **MIDI input is spread over the audio blocks.** When a DAW sends many notes at once (a chord dump, a burst), DUELL now takes at most 16 MIDI events per 0.7 ms and the rest right after, in their order. MIDI clock, Start, Stop and All Notes Off are never held back.
- **An overload is caught one step sooner.** When the processor misses one audio block, DUELL now fades out the two oldest release tails straight away instead of waiting for a second overloaded block. Held notes are only taken away, as before, when the load stays high.

### Compatibility
- Settings, patches, multis and patterns keep their format. The guide and MODE RECALL each use one unused bit of the settings; settings from older builds read as "guide not seen yet" and MODE RECALL OFF.

## DUELL 0.2.10 beta

### Removed
- **MOMENTARY is gone.** Holding LFO and turning a knob used to change a value only until LFO was let go. Holding LFO now opens QUICK LFO (below), and a turn there stays.

### Added
- **Quick edit layers: hold EDIT, LFO, ENV or ARP.** Hold one of these buttons for a moment (the same time as the other hold layers, MENU > HOLD) and the knobs get four performance controls of the selected part, shown over the page you are on. Let go and you are back. A short press still opens the page as before. QUICK FILTER (EDIT): CUT, RES, MODE and the amp envelope's REL. QUICK LFO: LFO 1 RATE, LFO 1 to cutoff, LFO 2 RATE, LFO 2 to cutoff (a synced LFO shows its SYNC instead of RATE). QUICK ENV: amp ATK, amp REL, ENV 2 to cutoff, ENV 2 DEC. QUICK ARP: MODE, RATE, GATE, OCT. The cards are the same as on the pages, the moves are recorded like any knob move, and MIDI LEARN picks them as usual.
- **In QUICK LFO, ENV and ARP the keys keep playing.** You can hold the button with one hand and play with the other. On engines with their own filter, QUICK FILTER uses it (ANALOG: CUT, RES, TYPE; TRIO: CUT, RES, MODE; NOISE: FREQ and RES).
- **The multimode filter on ANALOG, TRIO and NOISE too.** The FILTER page (CUT, RES, MODE, SLOPE) is now on every engine. ANALOG, TRIO and NOISE keep their own filters exactly as they are, but their pages are renamed so that only the multimode one is called FILTER: VCF (ANALOG), TRIO FILTER and NOISE FILT. On these three engines the multimode filter works after their own one and is switched off by default (MODE on LP and CUT fully open), so every saved sound plays exactly as before and it uses no CPU until you turn it on. On them the envelope, LFO and matrix routes to the cutoff, and MIDI CC74 and CC71, still move the engine's own filter; the multimode filter is set with its knobs (and the matrix's FRES moves its RES).
- **MORPH rolls the whole multimode filter.** On every engine, SMART and WILD now roll CUT, RES, MODE (low-pass through band-pass to high-pass) and SLOPE, and SMART may switch the filter on even if it was off. A roll never ends silent or dead: the cutoff stays in a range that suits the rolled mode, and the resonance stays musical. The two slopes cross-fade smoothly on the fader. Because of this, the random End B of 40 factory sounds was re-picked (a different seed) so that each still keeps its loudness; CROONER and SMOKE RING also use a smaller amount.

### Changed
- **16 factory sounds have new names.** The sounds themselves are unchanged. FANFARE is now FUNFARE, HORN SOLO is HAN SOLO, OBOE is OBOE-ISH, CLARINET is LICORICE, SOLO FLUTE is PURPLE FLUTE, HARPSICHORD is FAUX HARPSI, TOY PIANO is NURSERY KEYS, FLOOR TOM is FLOOR THUD, COWBELL is FIELD CLANK, ENSEMBLE is STRING MACH, KOTO is FAKE KOTO, STEEL DRUM is ISLAND STEEL, MUSIC BOX is NIGHT BOX, BEATBOX is VOCAL BEATS, FINGER BASS is PLUCK BASS and CHIMES is WAVE CHIMES. PATCHES is sorted by name within each category and engine, so some numbers moved: #31 HOOVER, #32 LICORICE, #56 ISLAND STEEL, #57 MALLET WOOD, #65 NURSERY KEYS, #66 NYLON GUT, #79 DRAWBARS, #80 PLATE BELL, #81 SYNTH BRASS, #82 WAVE CHIMES. The whole list with its numbers is in docs/PATCH_NUMBERS.md.
- **#101 FROZEN AIR (WAVE) is softer on top:** a low-pass at about 3 kHz with a little resonance, whose cutoff both LFOs now drift slowly and differently; the level is kept. Its End B is new: a new seed and a smaller amount (17 % instead of 31 %), so End B stays about as loud as the sound now that MORPH also rolls the filter's MODE and SLOPE.
- **PERFORM: PRESETS changes the sound again, and BALANCE is on SELECT.** On the PERFORM page PRESETS is back to its normal job: it changes the sound of the part ALGORITHM picked (with BOTH, each part moves one step on from its own sound, so the two stay different), and the footer shows the sound's name again, as on every other page. The balance between part 1 and part 2 is now SELECT, which has nothing else to do on that page. It works as before (the same range, centre and header message), and the top line of the footer reads SELECT: BALANCE.
- **OCT- and a knob: reset to the default.** Hold OCT- and turn a knob and that knob's card goes back to its default (PERFORM: MORPH to A, SPACE and FILTER to 0, DEPTH to 100; MIXER: LEVEL, PAN centre, SPACE 0; MODE: BAL centre; TEMPO and every plain parameter: its default value). The header says RESET and the card name once, and the knob is ignored until it stops for a moment. Because of this, OCT- and OCT+ now change the octave when you let go of them, not when you press; OCT- used for a reset does not change the octave. It also works while EDIT, LFO, ENV or ARP is held (the QUICK layers): there OCT- used for a reset does not put the values back either; a plain OCT- press still does. Dialogs, menus and action pages are as before.
- **Holding EDIT no longer changes the engine or the sound.** Its knobs used to be ENGINE, SOUND and FAV; these are on the ENGINE page (EDIT's first page). Holding EDIT is now QUICK FILTER. INIT on the first white key and the drum lane mutes on the black keys are as before, and OCT- still puts back what you changed while holding EDIT.
- **Every hold layer says QUICK.** The header of a hold layer shows a small lightning bolt and QUICK before its name, in the accent colour: QUICK FILTER, QUICK LFO, QUICK ENV, QUICK ARP, QUICK SCALE, QUICK SEQ, QUICK REC. So it never looks like a normal page.
- **Scale lights: the root dips, and MENU > DISPLAY > LEDS sets the keys' brightness too.** The root keys are lit brighter than the other keys of the scale and slowly dip to about half and back (a little under once a second), so they can be told apart at a glance; a key you hold or that is sounding is steady. The LEDS setting is now one brightness for the keys as well: OFF and LOW light the scale faintly (its keys at 10 %, the root at 25 %), LOW also lights the keys you play at half; MEDIUM lights the scale's keys at 25 %, the root at 60 % and the keys you play fully; MAX lights the scale's keys at 40 %, the root fully, and the keys you play go dark (MAX's look, also without a scale). With OFF-SCALE KEYS on PLAY the keys outside the scale glow faintly (with MAX at 12.5 %); with SNAP they stay dark.
- **REC is the HOLD switch outside the sequencer, with the arp off too.** On every page except the sequencer's own (STEP, AUTOMATION and the SEQ layer, where REC records as before), a tap of REC switches the selected part's HOLD (arp on: OFF and ARP, from ALL to OFF; arp off: OFF and ALL), the header says HOLD ON or HOLD OFF, and the REC light shows whether HOLD is on. A recording that is already armed stays armed (the REC mark in the header still shows it) and is armed or disarmed only on the sequencer's pages.
- **The MIXER's third card is SPACE instead of REV.** It is the same SPACE as PERFORM's second knob (one value for the whole instrument, -100 to +100), so you can use SPACE without opening PERFORM, and it works on every page. SPACE now keeps its value when you enter or leave PERFORM (before, it went back to 0). The part's reverb send REV is still on the FX page, knob 4. SPACE cannot be learned by MIDI LEARN.
- **ALGORITHM sets the MORPH target on the MORPH page.** On the MORPH page (HOME's first page) and on the PERFORM page, ALGORITHM now has three positions, from left to right: PART 1, BOTH, PART 2. With BOTH the morph (the fader, ROLL, UNDO, AMOUNT, and PERFORM's MORPH and ROLL keys) works on both parts, and both part numbers in the header are lit. It is one setting shared by the MORPH page and PERFORM: leaving and coming back keeps it.
- **On every other page ALGORITHM still just picks part 1 or part 2,** and the header shows only that part's number. If BOTH was set on the MORPH page, it stays set for the MORPH page and PERFORM; the other pages edit the part you picked last.
- **MORPH SET has no TARGET knob any more.** Its knobs are now K1 SMART, K2 ARP, K3 PITCH; K4 is empty. MIDI CC 111 still sets PART 1, PART 2 or BOTH. Holding SAVE with BOTH saves both parts only on the MORPH page (before: on MORPH SET too).
- **BOTH is not saved.** After power-on the morph works on one part. Settings, multis and projects keep their format: older ones that had TARGET BOTH load fine, and that setting is simply not used.
- **The header is calmer.** The multi number with its asterisk and the REC mark are gone from the header, on every page. Only the part badges stay at the left, and the page name has the room.
- **Armed recording shows at the bottom.** While a part is armed for recording, a red dot and the word REC appear in the footer's lower row, just left of the page number, on every page. A filled dot means the selected part is armed; an outlined dot means only the other part is. Arming and disarming still happens only on the sequencer's pages (STEP, AUTOMATION and the SEQ layer); everywhere else REC is the HOLD switch and an armed recording stays armed.
- **PLAY/STOP is green when stopped and red when playing.** Press it on green to play, on red to stop. During the count-in it blinks red on each beat. Before, it was lit green only while playing.
- **The REC light on other pages shows HOLD.** It is lit fully when HOLD is on and at the dim idle level when it is off. On the sequencer's pages it shows the armed recording as before.

### Fixed
- **A stored step pattern follows the scale.** The step sequencer now snaps its notes to the global scale when it plays them (OFF-SCALE SNAP; PLAY and CHROMATIC leave them as they are), so a pattern follows a new SCALE or ROOT. The stored notes are not changed. Drums and MIDI are not affected.
- **Chord keys under the arp follow a new scale.** If the SCALE or ROOT changes while you hold chord keys with the arp on, the arp now plays the new chord (C major to C minor, DIA3 on D4: D F Ab, not D F A) and keeps its place in the phrase.
- **PLAY/STOP no longer starts the sequencer before STOP ALL.** When stopped, a short press now starts on release; holding it for 0.6 s is STOP ALL at once and nothing starts. When running, a press still stops at once.

## DUELL 0.2.9 beta

### Removed
- **The sample engines SAMPLE, GRAIN and SLICE are removed.** Their built-in samples were small and could not do much, and the new WAVE engine replaces them. Their code and their samples (piano, flute, sax and the SLICE drum break) are gone, which leaves roughly 145 KB of the firmware's space for new things. The engine list now has ten engines: ANALOG, FM6, PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, TRIO, WHEEL, PHYS, NOISE and WAVE. Their 21 factory sounds were replaced by 21 new sounds on the other engines (below). Saved patches, multis and the autosave that use one of the removed engines stay as they were saved, but are silent: the screen says NO ENGINE once, the engine shows as "-", and the knobs of its EDIT pages do nothing. Turn ENGINE to choose another engine, or erase the patch in PATCHES (it is still listed under ALL). The editor no longer uploads samples (the user sample slots USR1 and USR2 are gone; their place in the memory is kept unused for future wavetables). A backup that holds samples still restores everything else.

### Added
- **A small CPU meter in the header.** Under the battery, on every page that has the header, a 5 px bar shows how busy the sound engine is, measured over the last second. It has 8 steps and is in the theme colour while all is well, amber from 70 % and red from 85 %. If the audio ever misses a block, the whole bar turns red for a second. It takes no room from the page name or the octave display, and nothing changes in the sound. MENU > DISPLAY > CPU METER turns it ON (the default, also for saved settings) or OFF. The web editor lists the new setting; the emulator shows its estimate, as ABOUT does.
- **A real multimode filter on EDIT > FILTER.** On the engines without a filter of their own (PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, PHYS, FM6, WAVE) the FILTER page now has four knobs: CUT, RES, MODE and SLOPE. CUT is the cutoff, shown in Hz, from 30 Hz up to 16 kHz. MODE blends smoothly from low-pass (LP) through band-pass (BP) to high-pass (HP); in between the card shows LP>BP or BP>HP. SLOPE is 12 dB or 24 dB per octave (24 dB is steeper and uses a little more CPU, only while the filter is working). A fully closed filter is silent: over the last 4 steps at the closed end the sound fades out completely (for the low-pass at 30 Hz, for the high-pass at 16 kHz). With MODE on LP and CUT fully open, which is the default, the filter is switched off and the sound is exactly as without it. The envelope, LFO and matrix routes to the cutoff, and the matrix's FRES, work as before. Turning MODE or SLOPE while a sound plays does not click.
- **21 new factory sounds** in place of the removed ones, in the same categories: PERC: DEEP KICK (PHASE), FLOOR TOM (PHYS), COWBELL (TRIO); FX: FROZEN AIR (WAVE), REV CYMBAL (NOISE), SONAR PING (PHASE); PLUCK: TOY PIANO (PHYS), STEEL DRUM (FM6), KOTO (PHASE); KEYS: HARPSICHORD (PHYS), SALOON KEYS (FM6); LEAD: SOLO FLUTE (WAVE), CLARINET (WAVE), OBOE (TRIO); PAD: ENSEMBLE (TRIO), WHISPER PAD (VOICE), BRASS PAD (WAVE); BASS: FINGER BASS (PHYS), FRETLESS (FM6); OTHER: DUSTY DRONE (LOFI), DIAL TONE (TRIO). There are still 114 factory sounds. Because sounds left and came, the numbers of most sounds in the PATCHES list moved.

### Changed
- **Scale lights: the root is steady and the other scale keys are brighter.** The root keys no longer pulse; they are lit steadily at about 75 % of full. The other keys of the scale are lit at about 40 % of full (before: 25 %), in every LEDS mode. The root is clearly brighter than the other scale keys, and a key you hold or that is sounding is still lit fully. Keys outside the scale are unchanged.
- **HOLD has a third value, ALL: the keys latch with the arp off, for pads and drones.** HOLD (ARP 1/3) now reads OFF, ARP or ALL. ARP is the old ON and works as before; ALL keeps a played chord sounding after the keys are released, with the arp off too (keys and MIDI), the next chord replaces it, and OFF or STOP ALL ends it. Each part holds its own chord. Older patches, multis and projects load unchanged.
- **REC is also the HOLD switch.** When the selected part's arp is on, or on the PERFORM page, a tap of REC switches that part's HOLD (arp on: OFF and ARP; PERFORM with the arp off: OFF and ALL), the header says HOLD ON or HOLD OFF, and the REC light shows whether HOLD is on. On the sequencer's own pages (STEP, AUTOMATION and the SEQ layer) REC records as before, and so it does on every other page while the arp is off.
- **The ARP pages are in a new order.** ARP 1/3 is MODE, HOLD, RATE, GATE; ARP 2/3 is SWG, PROB, OCT, ORD; ARP 3/3 is TRGT. With the arp MODE on SEQUENCER, the ARP pages show the row of sequence dots at the bottom with the step the arp is playing.
- **EQ is ordered by where to cut, then how much.** EQ 1/2 is LOW Hz, LOW dB, HIGH Hz, HIGH dB; EQ 2/2 is MID Hz, MID Q, MID dB.
- **DUCK and DELAY pages.** DUCK 1/2 is SRC, RATE (or THR or NOTE), DEPTH; DUCK 2/2 is ATK, RLS, CURVE. DELAY 2/2 is PING, SPREAD, RATE, DEPTH (RATE before DEPTH, as on the chorus and the LFOs).
- **LFO pages.** LFO 1 and LFO 2 are WAVE, RATE, SYNC, FADE; the new LFO 1 TRIG and LFO 2 TRIG pages are TRIG, PHS, POL. LFO 2's RATE is shown faded while LFO 2's SYNC is on, as LFO 1's already was.
- **AMP DEST is PIT, FLT, SHP, ESYNC**, the same order as the LFO DEST pages and the matrix.
- **GLO pages: GLO, TEMPO, SIDECHAIN, MIDI, MIDI LEARN.** The TEMPO page is now TEMPO, TAP, SWG, CLK: the clock source moved there from the MIDI page, and TAP is on K2.
- **EDIT has two VOICE pages.** VOICE 1/2 is VCE, GLD, GLMOD, PRIO; VOICE 2/2 is DTUNE, SPRD, ALLOC, PAN. MUTE is on the GLO page and the MIXER only. Saved sounds, multis and projects are not affected by any of the page changes.
- **The filter's CUT knob is no longer OFF in the middle.** It used to be one knob with a low-pass to the left and a high-pass to the right; that is now MODE plus CUT. Saved patches, multis, projects and the last session are converted when they load and sound the same: a low-pass at exactly the same cutoff, a high-pass as MODE HP at practically the same cutoff, OFF as the filter fully open. Their recorded CUT automation is converted too. The five factory sounds of 0.2.8 that use the filter (EVOLVING PAD, REESE BASS, HOOVER, DIGITAL PAD, BREATH PAD) sound exactly as before; only their MORPH End B moves the cutoff a little differently (the CUT knob has 82 steps now, and a random End B that used to cross the old middle into a high-pass now stays a low-pass).
- **CC74 covers the whole CUT range.** On these engines MIDI CC74 (and a MIDI LEARN map on CUT) now goes from 30 Hz at 0 to fully open at 127; 64 is no longer OFF. CC71 is still RES.
- **MORPH and the filter.** In SMART a filter that is off stays off (as before), CUT stays away from the silent ends (a low-pass not below about 130 Hz, a high-pass not above about 1.3 kHz, unless the sound is already there), and MODE and SLOPE never change, also in WILD.
- **PERFORM's FILTER closes completely.** K3 FILTER's low-pass now goes all the way down to 30 Hz (it stopped at 120 Hz) and the high-pass up to 15 kHz (it stopped at 3.8 kHz), and over the last 4 steps at each end the sound fades to silence. In the middle it is still exactly OFF.
- **The web editor** shows the new FILTER page under EDIT (CUT in Hz, MODE as LP / BP / HP) and converts an older library sound before it plays it on the device.
- **SPACE no longer changes the delay time; it changes the delay level and feedback.** Turning PERFORM's SPACE used to stretch or shorten the delay time slowly, which bent the pitch of the echoes while it moved and sounded weird. Now the echoes stay in time and in tune. SPACE still makes the chorus, delay and reverb smaller to the left and bigger to the right: their levels, the reverb's length, the delay's feedback and the chorus depth. At the far right the chorus depth now goes only up to the DEPTH knob's own maximum (it was twice that, an audible pitch wobble). In the middle everything sounds exactly as before.
- **SPACE now also adds reverb, delay and chorus when the sends are at 0.** Turning SPACE to the right used to only multiply each part's sends, so a sound with its sends at 0 got no space at all. Now, turning right, every part sends at least a minimum amount to the reverb, delay and chorus, which grows the further right you turn (at the far right at least as much as a reverb send of 76, a delay send of 46 and a chorus send of 30; less into the SHIM and RESO reverbs, which would otherwise clip). A part whose own send is already higher keeps its own, raised by SPACE as before. In DUEL each part only goes into its own effects. Turning SPACE to the left still only shrinks what is there.
- **SPACE also sets the stereo width.** Turned to the left it narrows the sound down to completely mono at the far left; turned to the right it widens it (without making it louder). A sound that is mono stays mono. In DUEL the left and right outputs are the two parts, so the width is left out there (the rest of SPACE works) and the header says DUEL: NO WIDTH the first time you turn SPACE.
- **The row of sequence dots at the bottom now shows only on the sequencer pages** (STEP, AUTOMATION and SEQ TOOLS). On the other pages that row is empty, except where it already showed something else (MIDI LEARN, PERFORM, the PATTERNS and MULTIS save hint).
- **ABOUT: KNOB 1 scrolls, SELECT jumps between sections.** On MENU > SYSTEM > ABOUT, turn KNOB 1 right to scroll down through the information, credits and licences and left to scroll back up. SELECT now jumps to the next or previous section (the animation, the information, CREDITS, LICENSES), and OCT- goes back to the menu. Before, SELECT scrolled and turning a knob left left the page.
- **Factory multi M03 DEAD BUFFER:** part 1 played #2 STRETCH on the removed GRAIN engine; it now plays FROZEN AIR (WAVE), a slowly scanned airy texture with the same envelopes, movement and effects. Everything else of M03 is as it was: part 2 (MODEM), your own changes to part 1 (the auto-pan, the drive), the effects, the mode, the tempo and End B. If you saved over M03 yourself, your saved version is kept and its part 1 is silent until you choose a sound for it.
- **ERASE DATA's dialog** now says the FM6 voice bank is kept (it said user samples).

### Fixed
- **SPACE turned to the right no longer glitches.** While SPACE moved, the chorus jumped a little on every audio block, a fast crackle on any sound with chorus (the same happened when the chorus DEPTH was turned or moved by the matrix); the SPRING reverb did the same while its size changed. Both now move smoothly. With the RESO reverb, SPACE at the right made its four resonators ring into their limit and clip hard; there SPACE now raises the sustain only part of the way and leaves RESO's input level as it is, so it stays clean.

## DUELL 0.2.8 beta

### Added
- **More modulation destinations, and the matrix says what they are.** The matrix (MATRIX 1-4 / 5-8) can now also move the part's LEVEL, the INSERT's three knobs and its MIX, the EQ's LOW, MID and HIGH gains, DUCK DEPTH, ENV 2's ATK, DEC, SUS, REL and its FLT and SHP depths, the filter's RES (on the engines with the universal filter), and the effects: CHORUS RATE and DEPTH, DELAY FDBK, the delay modulation's DEPTH and RATE, REVERB SIZE and DAMP. The effects are shared in STEREO and LAYER, so there the routes of both parts add up; in DUEL each part has its own effects and moves only its own. The engine's own knobs (on WAVE: POS, BEND, SYNC, SUB, DTN) and LFO 1 / LFO 2 RATE and DEPTH were destinations already; now a line under the four slots names the chosen destination in full ("DEST: WAVE SYNC", "DEST: REVERB SIZE") and, for an effect, whose effect it moves. The card is labelled DEST (was DST). A destination that cannot work with the current choices is greyed with the reason and does nothing: an engine knob that is not used, WAVE's TABLE (a table change is a rebuild), FM6's SLOT, SAMPLE SET and SLICE SRC, DIV, START, REV (read only as a note starts), PAN in DUEL, the INSERT's knobs while the INSERT is OFF, RES on an engine with its own filter. The delay's TIME is not a destination (a jump in time would click; the delay modulation is the smooth way). Saved patches, multis and projects keep their routes exactly; nothing changes for a sound that does not use the new destinations.
- **Twelve new WAVE tables and six new sounds.** TABLE now goes on after ROUGH with BAND, DUAL, THIN and PIPE (classic digital sweeps in the spirit of the 1980s wavetable synths, and a church organ) and REESE, HOOVR, GROWL, WOBBL, YELL, METAL, STEPS and AIR (modern ones: phasing saws, the hoover, a growl, a wobble filter, a scream, a metal plate, a stepped low-sample-rate sine, a flute with breath). Like the others they are made from short recipes when chosen and stay clean on high notes; the first 14 tables are unchanged, so saved sounds play as before. New factory sounds: GROWL BASS, REESE BASS, HOOVER, DIGITAL PAD, BREATH PAD and PLATE BELL (114 factory sounds now; they sit with the other WAVE sounds at the end of their categories, so the list places after them move).

### Changed
- **Every WAVE sound now also moves a second parameter (BEND, DTN or SUB) through the matrix**, beside the table position it already moved (BREATH PAD, which did not move its table position by itself, now drifts it slowly). Two sounds were made a little louder to match (DIGI PLUCK, HOOVER) and BREATH PAD has a new End B seed.
- **The PERFORM page no longer shows the patch name** at the bottom. There PRESETS changes BALANCE, not the sound, so the footer now shows only the engine (its icon and name) under the PRESETS: BALANCE and ALGO line, which makes it clear that you are not choosing presets in this mode.
- **Scale lights: the root now stands out.** The scale's keys are lit at a quarter of full (the MEDIUM idle level) instead of about half, and the root keys pulse slowly between full and about 60 % (a little under once a second) while the scale is shown. A key you hold or that is sounding is lit fully and steadily, the root too. The DRUM grid's beat keys with LEDS MEDIUM stay at about half.
- **PERFORM's DRIVE is now a fuzz.** It was a wave-fold into a hard clip, which dropped the note itself and got very bright at higher DEPTH. It is now a high-gain, lopsided diode-style clip with a tone filter, level-matched, K4 DEPTH sets how hard. (The firmware can still be built with the fold, tube-style or crush model for comparison.)
- **A little slow movement in some factory sounds.** About 15 factory sounds on the original engines have a little slow movement now (a few percent of an LFO or envelope on a tone control); they are the same sounds otherwise. (Eleven of them have a new End B seed, so MORPH's End B stays within the usual loudness and brightness range of the sound.)

## DUELL 0.2.5 beta

**Scale lights and delay modulation are new, the filter works at CUT OFF, the pump is tuned, SPLIT is now called DUEL, and USB LEVEL FIXED no longer overdrives the limiter.**

### Added
- **PERFORM mode.** Hold FX for a moment (or hold FX and press a white key) and the 16 white keys become performance effects that last while you hold them: REPEAT 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, REVERSE, TAPE STOP, FREEZE, OCT UP, OCT DN, FLANGER, PHASER, DRIVE, PUMP (the whole mix ducks on every beat), ROLL A and ROLL B (a new MORPH end) and MUTE 1 and MUTE 2. The PERFORM page shows the keys and four knobs: MORPH, SPACE (the delay, reverb and chorus smaller to the left, huge to the right), FILTER (low-pass left, high-pass right, OFF in the middle) and DEPTH (how strong the effects are); there PRESETS is BALANCE and ALGORITHM picks PART 1, PART 2 or BOTH. A tap of FX goes back. Nothing about it is saved, and with no key held and the knobs in the middle the sound is exactly as before. A tap of FX still opens the FX pages.
- **Scale lights are automatic.** When the global SCALE is not CHROMATIC, the keys now show it by themselves: the scale's keys are lit about half bright, the root keys fully lit, the keys outside the scale dark (or, with OFF-SCALE KEYS on PLAY, a faint glow), and the key you press or the note that sounds lights fully. No menu step is needed; MENU > CONTROL > SCALE LEDS is now AUTO (default) or OFF. With CHROMATIC the keys look as before. Saved settings load as AUTO.
- **Delay modulation.** DELAY 2/2 has two new knobs, DEPTH and RATE: the delay gets its own small LFO that gently wobbles the delay time, like a tape or a chorus, with the left and right repeats moving out of step so the delay also sounds wider in STEREO and LAYER (in DUEL each part's delay just wobbles). DEPTH 0 is off and the default: every existing sound and saved project plays exactly as before. RATE is faded while DEPTH is 0. It needs no extra memory and costs nothing while off.
- **WAVE, a wavetable engine.** A new engine, after SLICE in the engine list. TABLE picks one of 14 tables (saw to square, pulse width, harmonics, odd harmonics, organ drawbars, vowels, brass, bowed string, resonant sweep, comb, FM digital, glass, chimes, rough), POS moves through it, BEND bends the wave's shape, SYNC adds hard sync, SUB a sine an octave down, DTN a second detuned copy. The envelopes, the LFOs and the matrix move POS through their SHP depths; CUT and RES are the universal filter, as on PHASE. A table is made from a short recipe the moment it is chosen, so the part is silent for a moment (usually a few hundredths of a second) and then fades in; high notes stay clean (no harsh aliasing). Eleven new factory sounds: PWM PAD, VOWEL PAD, GLASS PAD, EVOLVING PAD, DRAWBARS, CHIMES, SYNTH BRASS, DIGI PLUCK, SYNC LEAD, HARM BASS and RESO SWEEP (108 factory sounds now; they come last in their categories, so the list places after them move). Every other engine, sound and saved setting plays as before.

### Changed
- **The universal filter now works with CUT at OFF.** On PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, GRAIN, PHYS, FM6 and SLICE, an envelope, an LFO, the modulation matrix or aftertouch routed to the filter now moves it even when the CUT knob is in the centre (OFF); before, the routes did nothing until you turned CUT away from OFF. The filter adds the knob and all the modulation together: below zero it is a low-pass closing, above zero a high-pass, as on the knob. It is bypassed only when the total is exactly zero, so a sound with CUT OFF and no filter route is unchanged, bit for bit. The ENV, LFO and ENV 2 / LFO 2 FLT cards and RES are no longer greyed out on these engines (SET CUT is gone), and the delay modulation's RATE is no longer greyed at DEPTH 0 (SET DEP is gone): a control is greyed only when a selection makes it impossible, never because its value is zero. CPU: when only a route is active the filter costs the same as with the knob on (about 44 instructions per voice and sample on PHASE and WHEEL). Factory sounds keep their sound: on the eight universal-filter engines their FLT routes (which did nothing while CUT was OFF) now start at 0, so every factory sound plays exactly as in 0.2.4; for 16 of them the End B seed was re-picked (the roll draws changed with the removed routes). A MORPH roll still never switches the filter on by itself when CUT is OFF: SMART keeps a FLT depth at 0 and adds no CUT route (WILD may).
- **The LEDS modes are now called OFF, LOW, MEDIUM, MAX** (before: OFF, DIM LO, DIM HI, INV). MEDIUM is still the default, MAX is still the stock look, and saved settings and the editor keep working. MEDIUM is now clearly brighter: the idle buttons and keys are lit a quarter of the time (25 % of full) instead of a faint glow, LOW stays the faintest glow (under 2 %).
- **Scale lights tuned.** The scale's keys are now lit at about half of full, clearly dimmer than the root (full) and well above MEDIUM. Keys outside the scale follow OFF-SCALE KEYS: with SNAP they stay dark, with PLAY (they play their own note) they glow faintly at the LOW level so you can see them. The DRUM grid's beat keys with LEDS MEDIUM are lit at the same 5/6 level, above the MEDIUM keys.
- **The ducker's pump is tuned for house and techno (120 to 140 BPM).** Turn DEPTH up on a part with the built-in PUMP source and nothing else touched: it ducks on every quarter note and the sound comes back the way a compressor lets go, fast at first and then settling smoothly (CURVE now defaults to EXP; LIN, the straight-line release, is still a choice). HOLD is gone from the DUCK pages (a pump is just attack and release; DUCK 2/2 is RLS and CURVE); an old stored HOLD value is kept but no longer does anything. PUMP now keeps counting the beats while DEPTH is 0, so raising DEPTH later ducks on the beat of the transport and not at the moment you turn the knob. Saved patches, multis and projects keep the curve they had; the nine factory sounds that use the ducker now get the smoother EXP release. The normal (SMART) morph roll of a part with the ducker on now moves only DEPTH, so the tuned attack, release and threshold stay; WILD still rolls them. This also changes the End B of those nine sounds (they keep their seeds).
- The dual-mono output mode SPLIT is now called DUEL, because LAYER has a key SPLIT. Only the name changed: saved data and MIDI are unaffected.

### Fixed
- USB LEVEL FIXED no longer overdrives the limiter; USB now carries the mix at the default MASTER level. The speaker and headphones still follow MASTER (above the default position the loudest peaks are clipped at full scale, never wrapped).

## DUELL 0.2.4 beta (first firmware beta)

**The first firmware release: the device package `duell-0.2.4-beta.fwsc`, the installer page and the browser emulator `duell-emulator-0.2.4-beta.zip`. It covers everything since 0.1 beta (the earlier builds 0.2.1 to 0.2.3 were never published). Tried on one FM-1, the author's; install at your own risk; no warranty; not affiliated with M-VAVE.**

### New (since 0.1 beta)
- **Chords and one global scale.** SCALE and ROOT are now device settings (default CHROMATIC, root C), not part of a patch, part, multi or factory sound. SCL button, page 1 SCALE (K1 SCALE, K2 ROOT, K3 OFF-SCALE) and page 2 CHORD (K1 CHRD, K2 VOIC, K3 TRN, the selected part's). OFF-SCALE KEYS: SNAP (default) moves a key or MIDI note outside the scale to the nearest scale note (a tie goes down), PLAY lets it pass. Both parts sound the same note for a key. DIA3 / DIA7 chords stack the scale's degrees; with the scale CHROMATIC they play a plain MAJ triad / MAJ7 and the card says so. The root of a chord is never re-pitched.
- **Universal filter** for the engines that had none of their own (PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, GRAIN, PHYS, FM6, SLICE), on a new EDIT > FILTER page: K1 CUT is one bipolar knob (centre OFF, left a low-pass, right a high-pass), K2 RES. Envelopes, LFOs, the modulation matrix and MIDI CC74 (CUT, 64 = OFF) and CC71 (RES) drive it; while CUT is OFF the sound is unchanged. ANALOG, TRIO, NOISE and SAMPLE keep their own filters.
- **SAVE flow.** A short press of SAVE opens the page that fits: PATTERNS from a sequencer page (STEP, GRID, AUTOMATION), otherwise MULTIS in LAYER and PATCHES in SPLIT and STEREO. **Hold SAVE saves over** the patch you are editing (BOTH patches on the MORPH page), and now also over the pattern or the multi picked on the PATTERNS and MULTIS lists ("SAVE OVER <name>?", OCT+ yes; an empty multi asks for a name first). Page buttons leave and cancel any dialog without writing. The PATCHES list shows at most three empty places at its end; FREE nn still shows the true number.
- **ERASE DATA** (MENU > SYSTEM > ERASE DATA, OCT+ asks, OCT+ yes) erases settings, favourites, autosave, your patches, multis and pattern slots (not your user samples) and restarts the unit as new (VIOLET, LAYER, M01). The installer has an optional tick box "Erase my saved data after installing" (set by what the unit runs, see Changed). A plain reinstall still keeps your data.
- **GLO page.** GLO is now GLO, MIDI, SIDECHAIN, MIDI LEARN, TEMPO. The keys play notes there. K1 MUTE 1 and K2 MUTE 2 act on one click (before: three clicks), K3 LEVEL 1, K4 LEVEL 2, with a meter per part; new TEMPO page (tempo, swing, tap) and MIDI page (channels, MIDI IN, clock source). MORPH's ROLL also acts on one click (A left, B right). Every part starts unmuted at power-on.
- **VIOLET theme** (the default) is now the darker, near-black, more saturated violet and pink.
- **Menu.** Page buttons (EDIT, FX, ENV, LFO, ARP, SEQ, SCL, GLO, SAVE) now work inside the MENU and close it. Menu settings are saved when they change (about 2 s after the knob rests). ABOUT shows the version, build date, USB link and CPU line.
- **Header and footer redone:** the page name sits in the top middle of every screen; the footer shows the engine, the full patch name and the page number.
- **Factory sounds and multis.** Four new factory multis M01 EERIE SIGNAL, M02 WIRED SNAIL, M03 DEAD BUFFER, M04 ACID ETHER. Several factory sounds were revised after audition on the device: #19 HORN SOLO, #22 SILVER FLUTE (no click at the loop wrap), #65 MOTOR VIBES, #66 ROSEWOOD, #67 OPEN SIZZLE, #68 STATIC SNARE, #72 WOOFER, #97 renamed SNARE BUZZ. LFO TRIG now defaults to FREE. Because of the new filter, 46 factory sounds on the filterless engines no longer move their tone controls from the cutoff routes (to be checked by ear).
- **Installer page.** BACK UP, INSTALL DUELL and RETURN TO OFFICIAL FIRMWARE are three separate cards with their own progress, a banner names the running action, the other buttons are locked meanwhile and closing the tab asks first.
- **One version source** (the `VERSION` file): the device (splash, ABOUT), the installer and the emulator show "DUELL 0.2.4 beta".

### Changed or removed

- The FX hold layer's performance effects are gone (REPEAT, REVERSE, TAPE STOP, FREEZE, OCT UP / DN, master FLANGER and PHASER, CRUSH, THROW, the master FILTER macro, black-key track mutes, MENU > CONTROL > FX LATCH). A short press of FX still opens the FX pages, holding it is the same, and keys pressed with FX held are notes. The GLO mutes, the metronome, the per-part INSERT and the master chain are as before.
