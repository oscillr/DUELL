# Changelog

**DUELL**
MultiMorphing Bitimbral Synthesizer
by OSCILL/R

Each release: a one-line summary, then New, Fixed, Compatibility, Install, Files.

## DUELL 1.0

The first full release: everything of the 0.2.33 test beta, named 1.0 (no beta label on the unit, in the installer or in the editor). The firmware code is the one of the 0.2.33 test build T0233 apart from the version strings and one change: the release build runs part 2 on the second core by default.

- **New:** part 2 plays on core 1 from every power-on, with the automatic fallback to one core (a start that fails, a misread, a stall or a crash leaves that session or the next start on one core). The release build has no BENCH menu, no TEST screen and no USB serial console.
- **What 1.0 is:** the owner's 128 factory patches (levelled by TRIM, #25 DRIVEN SAW new), the owner's 15 factory multis with a MORPH End B rolled by the firmware's own ROLL B, one list of 32 patterns, the MULTI and TWIN filters, the update screen while the firmware installs (DUELL, WRITING, percent, a 20-segment bar, DO NOT SWITCH OFF), KEY VELOCITY (off by default) and AFTERTOUCH (on).
- **Install:** the package is `duell-1.0.fwsc` (identity FM-1_901); back up first, then use the installer page.

## DUELL 0.2.33 beta

A test beta (not 1.0): the 0.2.32 line with the owner's multis, a new lead, level balance, a loader screen while the firmware installs, and the installer and editor notes cleaned up.

- **New:** while the firmware is written the unit shows the DUELL screen: the DUELL word over the splash's wave lines, WRITING, the percent, a bar of 20 segments and DO NOT SWITCH OFF (the update loader draws it; if the panel does not answer, the loader writes exactly as before). The factory multis M01 to M05 and M08 to M15 are the owner's records; M01 to M15 have a new MORPH End B (the firmware's own ROLL B, fixed seeds). New patch #25 DRIVEN SAW (an aggressive twin-filter lead) replaces CROONER.
- **Changed:** the levels of the factory multis are balanced by TRIM (both parts of a multi by the same dB); #65 ARP PLUCK is one step quieter. The patches FUNFARE to PIXEL HERO are now #26 to #33 (one higher). The installer has one plain notice about installing at your own risk; the "saves may change" notes are gone.
- **Compatibility:** the VOICE engine's factory sounds were renumbered after CROONER left (a saved patch of VOICE number 2 or higher may show another sound). Back up first.
- **Install:** the package with the loader screen is `duell-0.2.33-beta.fwsc`; a package without it (same sources, DUELL_LOADER_SCREEN=0) has the name ending in `-noscreen`.

## DUELL 0.2.32 beta

A test beta (not 1.0): the new factory bank, one list of 32 patterns, the MULTI and TWIN filter names, TWIN OFFSET in semitones, true plus and minus 100 percent.

- **New:** the 128 factory patches are the owner's bank (levels balanced by TRIM alone); every patch stores and recalls its own arp (OFF too) and chord keys; one list of 32 patterns P01 to P32 (none empty, ERASE gives a slot its default back); the editor reads the 32 slots; the BLEND card shows MULTI / BOTH / TWIN; the TWIN CHAIN flow strip (two branches in PARALLEL); the menu lines KEY VELOCITY (OFF by default) and AFTERTOUCH (ON by default); the ENGINE selector is the first line of the ENGINE tab; the installer says "Done. Switch the unit off and on once."
- **Changed:** the two filters are called MULTI FILTER and TWIN FILTER everywhere; TWIN OFFSET is in whole semitones (-36 to +36) with LINK on; a depth at +63 acts as 100 percent (matrix amounts and the direct depth cards ENV / LFO to PIT, FLT, SHP and TWIN ENV 2 / LFO 1 / LFO 2; 0 and the negative side are unchanged); the matrix destinations DEPTH and DEPTH 2 are hidden (a stored route still plays).
- **Compatibility:** pattern objects changed format (PTS3): older saves with 16 pattern slots are refused once and the unit starts with the new bank and the 32 default patterns. Back up first.

### Added
- **Overload guard.** When the processor is near its limit (two audio blocks in a row over 85 %), the delay's RANDOM or OCT UP mode plays as the plain delay, faded over about 12 ms. When the CPU bar has stayed under 70 % for about 3 s, the events fade back in. Automatic and always on; nothing is saved and nothing changes below the limit. ABOUT shows LIGHT FX while it is on. In the host model it takes about 5 points off M16 STRESS TEST in LAYER and about 9 in DUEL.

- **WIDTH, the master stereo width (a page of the GLO button, right before BASS MONO).** One knob, **K1 WIDTH**, 0 to 200 %. **100 % is the default and means off:** the stage is not run and the sound is bit for bit what it was. Below 100 % the mix gets narrower, 0 % is mono; above 100 % it gets wider, 200 % makes the side twice as loud.
  - **The bass stays where it is.** Wider only acts above 125 Hz (a 12 dB/octave crossover), so the low end is never made wider. Narrower acts on everything, the bass too. BASS MONO comes after WIDTH, so when it is on, the low end below its corner stays mono whatever WIDTH does.
  - **Mono stays the same.** Only the side (the difference of left and right) is changed, never the middle: the mono sum of the mix is exactly what it was at every setting, and a mono sound stays mono.
  - **No click:** a turn glides over about 23 ms.
- **Both:** in the chain after the effects, SPACE and PERFORM: WIDTH, BASS MONO, COMP, then the MASTER volume, the limiter and the soft clip. The GLO pages follow that order: TEMPO, CHORUS, DELAY 1/2, DELAY 2/2, REVERB, SHIMMER (with TYPE SHIM), WIDTH, MASTER (BASS MONO), COMP, MODULES, SIDECHAIN, MIDI, MIDI LEARN. **STEREO and LAYER only:** in DUEL (part 1 left, part 2 right) WIDTH and BASS MONO do not run, their cards are not shown, and the page says NOT IN DUEL MODE and why. WIDTH belongs to the unit like COMP: kept when you power off, not saved in a patch, a multi or a project, and a load does not change it; ERASE DATA resets it. Not learnable by MIDI LEARN, not morphed. **Editor:** WIDTH is a card of the MASTER group of the GLOBAL tab (setting 42).
  - **Cost** (host instructions a sample, 33 = 1 %; the device reads about 0.75 to 0.9 of it): WIDTH about 38 (1.2 %), nothing at 100 %.
- **Load bench (for developers).** `./host_tests.sh bench` prints one table of what the processor pays for each engine's voice, each INSERT type, SAT, EQ, DUCK, each reverb type, delay mode, the chorus (in STEREO and in DUEL), the master stages, and a worst-case scene per MODE with the estimated number of voices a part before 100 %. It fails only when a figure grows more than 10 % over the stored baseline. See [docs/TESTING.md](docs/TESTING.md).
- **All sixteen MULTI slots hold a factory multi.** Fifteen are music, sorted so related ones sit together: M01 REESE SWAMP, M02 NIGHT ORGAN, M03 PAD SPLIT, M04 DUB CHORD (bass grooves and splits); M05 WHISPER TIDE, M06 TINE STRINGS, M07 FINAL REEL, M08 HAZY TENSOR (pads and layers); M09 ACID ETHER, M10 WIRED SNAIL, M11 EERIE SIGNAL, M12 DEAD BUFFER; M13 COLD CHAPEL, M14 GARGLE ACID, M15 WOOD & SKIN (for performing); M16 STRESS TEST. NIGHT ORGAN, PAD SPLIT, DUB CHORD, WHISPER TIDE, TINE STRINGS, HAZY TENSOR, ACID ETHER, DEAD BUFFER and GARGLE ACID are the owner's multis, byte for byte as he saved them (M02 to M06 from his backup of 2026-10-17). No tribute remains (BASILDON is spare). They use all ten engines, splits (NIGHT ORGAN, PAD SPLIT), MORPH, the ducker and the arps. The splits are applied when MULTI LOAD is set to SETS MODE; with KEEPS MODE (the default) every multi also plays in LAYER. Three multis made for 0.2.23 (VELVET TINES, SEVENOAKS and a drone) left the sixteen to make room; their records are kept as spare data (`felucca-base/tests/scenes/spare/`, listed in `tools/factory_multis.py` SPARE) and can come back.
- **M07 FINAL REEL is the owner's refined version.** Same two sounds (#58 BRASS PAD + #54 STRING MACH), LAYER, 71 BPM; he reworked the sounds' settings in the multi, the effects and the XFADE, and the keys no longer latch (HOLD OFF); imported byte for byte from his multi file (the importer reads the editor's MULTIS file now, tools/import_owner_edits.py).
- **M08 is the owner's HAZY TENSOR** (#3 LADDER BASS + #20 GEARBOX, LAYER, 117 BPM), byte for byte from his multi file. It replaces MONO DUEL (the owner: "Mono-dual is out"), which was the only factory multi in DUEL: no factory multi uses DUEL now. MONO DUEL's record is kept as spare data (`felucca-base/tests/scenes/spare/M08_MONO_DUEL.bin`, listed in `tools/factory_multis.py` SPARE) and can come back. The other multis are unchanged.
- **M16 STRESS TEST** is a test, not music: the two heaviest sounds with every stage on, so you can hear how many notes your unit takes before it clicks (docs/TESTING.md). It is set so that one key plays on the unit: the FLANG insert and SAT are on the whistle, the three-band EQ and the ducker on the pad, the pad's 1/16 arp has GATE 71 and a short release (REL 47), 24 dB filters on both, chorus, RANDOM delay at CHANCE 71 %, SHIM PITCH UP with MOD 51, LAYER, 137 BPM. Host model: 1 key 63 %, 2 keys 79 %, 3 keys and more 95 % (DUEL 72 / 87 / 104 %), so every unit should play one key and show its limit at two or three; the overload guard (below) takes about 5 points off in LAYER and 9 in DUEL. It can be overwritten; ERASE brings it back.
- **COMP, the master compressor (a page of the GLO button, right after MASTER).** A compressor on the whole output, for gluing the mix and taming loud peaks. The page has four knobs: **K1 THRESH** (-40 to 0 dB, default -17), **K2 RATIO** (1:1, 1.5:1, 2:1, 3:1, 4:1, 6:1, 8:1, 12:1, INF), **K3 ATTACK** (0.1 to 100 ms in 40 steps a decade, default 10) and **K4 RELEASE** (20 ms to 2 s in 40 steps a decade, default 200 ms).
  - **Off by default, and then it costs nothing.** RATIO 1:1 is the default and means the compressor is not run at all: the sound is bit for bit what it was (every factory multi renders the same). At 1:1 only the RATIO card is shown, because THRESH, ATTACK and RELEASE have nothing to act on; turn RATIO up and they appear.
  - **The makeup gain is automatic.** It is worked out from THRESH and RATIO: a level of -8 dB comes out as loud as it went in, quieter signals are raised a little, louder ones are brought down. So switching it on does not make the music louder: a signal at the limiter's threshold is never louder out than in, and in the three demo pieces the average loudness stayed within 1.3 dB of the uncompressed render. The makeup is at most +12 dB.
  - **How it works.** One compressor for both channels (the left and right are squeezed together, so the picture does not lean), a fixed soft knee of 6 dB, a peak detector with a hold of about 12 ms so a bass does not make it ripple, the gain updated every 0.18 ms. The times are the detector's time constants: ATTACK is the time to cover 63 % of a step up, RELEASE of a fall (the hold included); the test measures the times against what is printed. The dB of THRESH are those of the output with the MASTER knob at its default; MASTER itself is a volume after the compressor and does not change what is compressed.
  - **Where in the signal path:** after the effects, SPACE, PERFORM, WIDTH and BASS MONO before MASTER, the limiter and the soft clip. USB audio gets it too.
  - **STEREO and LAYER only.** In DUEL (part 1 left, part 2 right for an external mixer) a linked compressor would pump part 2 from part 1, so it does not run there and its cards are not shown. The values are kept for when you go back.
  - **No click.** Switching between 1:1 and a ratio fades over 15 ms, and a changed THRESH or RATIO glides.
  - **It belongs to the unit, not to a multi.** The settings are saved with the unit's other settings (device-wide, about 2 s after the last turn); a multi, a project or a patch does not hold them, and loading one does not change them. ERASE DATA resets it to 1:1. It cannot be learned by MIDI LEARN or morphed.
  - **Editor:** a COMP card on the GLOBAL tab, in the same MASTER card as BASS MONO (the settings 34 to 37; it hides THRESH, ATTACK and RELEASE at 1:1, as the unit does). The editor cannot see the mode, so it keeps showing the card, with a note that the unit does not use it in DUEL.
  - **Cost:** about 53 host instructions per sample while it runs (about 1.6 % of the model CPU, about 1.2 to 1.4 % on the device by the usual rule), nothing when RATIO is 1:1.
  - **Not built yet:** an OTT-style mode (a second, lower threshold that also lifts quiet parts) can be added later; the gain computer is written so it can take it.
- **KEY TRK on the multimode filter.** The cutoff follows the note you play. KTRK goes from -100 % to +100 %, **0 by default** (off). At +100 % the cutoff moves one octave for every octave of pitch, around MIDI note 60 (the same centre as the KTR on ANALOG's own filter); at -100 % it moves the other way. It is part of the sound: it is saved in patches, multis and projects, can be automated and learned by MIDI LEARN, and it rolls with the other filter values when you morph (it never switches the filter on by itself).
  - **0 is exactly the sound as before** (bit for bit): every factory sound and multi plays as in 0.2.20. A sound load sets KTRK to 0.
  - **With CUT at the open end,** a KTRK above 0 closes the notes below C3 (their cutoff falls under the top); the notes above stay open.
  - **Cost:** one multiply and an add per voice and block while KTRK is not 0, nothing at 0. The package is the same size.
  - **Stored:** on id 27, the old QNT's (retired on 2026-10-12; nothing read it). `P_COUNT` stays 127. A sound saved while QNT still existed and with a QNT above 0 reads as a few percent of KEY TRK.
- **MULTI FILTER 2/2.** The multimode filter has two pages now: **MULTI FILTER 1/2** is as before (CUT, RES, MODE, SLOPE) and **2/2** has four knobs: K1 **KTRK**, K2 **ENV 2**, K3 **LFO 1** and K4 **LFO 2**: how much ENV 2, LFO 1 and LFO 2 move the cutoff. There is no AMP ENV card here (envelope 1 is the amp envelope; the filter takes no depth from it by default; AMP DEST keeps its own FLT card). A depth connects two modules, so it is reachable at both places and it is one stored value: these are the same values as the FLT cards on ENV 2 DEST, LFO 1 DEST and LFO 2 DEST, and those cards are drawn on every engine. On the engines where the routes drive the multimode filter (PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, PHYS, FM6, WAVE) MULTI FILTER 2/2 shows them as well. On ANALOG, TRIO and NOISE they drive the engine's own filter, so there they are on the DEST pages only and 2/2 shows KTRK alone. QUICK MULTI is unchanged (CUT, RES, MODE, REL).
- **Editor:** the ENGINE tab's MULTI FILTER card has KTRK, and the three amounts ENV 2, LFO 1 and LFO 2 on the engines above; the MOD tab keeps them too. A SIDECHAIN card is on the MIDI tab of the GLOBAL column (see Changed).
- **New last FX page, SEND FX.** It shows what the delay and the reverb are set to, and a CHORUS card. Only the selected part's badge is lit. **K1 CHORUS** (the label only), **K2 DELAY** the mode with the time under it (for example RAND, 1/8), **K3 REVERB** the type with the size under it (for example PLATE, 71 %), K4 has no card. No send levels are shown: those are on the FX page. The cards are always there: when the selected part sends nothing to that effect (its send is 0, or MODULES SENDS OFF) the value is dim and the small line says INACTIVE. The delay shows its mode in DUEL too. Turning K1, K2 or K3 either way jumps to the CHORUS, DELAY 1/2 or REVERB page of GLO; no value changes, and the rest of the same turn is ignored for a moment so it does not move the first card of the page you land on.
- **RENAME in the SAVE dialog.** When you hold SAVE and the dialog asks "OVERWRITE #NN <name>?", turn **knob 2** (the hint line says K2 RENAME next to K1 OTHER SLOT): the NAME screen opens with the sound's name. OCT+ there returns to the dialog, which now reads WITH <new name>; OCT+ in the dialog saves the sound into the slot under that name; OCT- anywhere drops it. Not offered with TARGET BOTH (two sounds).
- **Hold GLO: LEVEL 1, PAN 1, LEVEL 2, PAN 2.** The pan of each part can be set from the panel now. The mutes moved to the keys: with GLO held, OCT- mutes or unmutes part 1 and OCT+ part 2 (no octave change from that press); the two cells say LIVE or MUTED and name the key.
- **A MASTER page (GLO, after WIDTH):** K1 BASS MONO, its one knob. **BASS MONO is a setting of the unit now, ON at 200 Hz on a new unit** (it was off, and it was saved with the project); a multi or a patch never changes it.
- **A third colour for the global pages.** TEMPO, CHORUS, DELAY, REVERB, SHIMMER, WIDTH, MASTER, COMP, MODULES, SIDECHAIN, MIDI and SCALE draw their values and gauges in a colour of their own (amber on VIOLET and COBALT, teal on ORANGE, violet on MINT, deep teal on PALE, orange on OCEAN, yellow on HI-CON), on the LAYER background, with both part badges lit as before. ALGORITHM does nothing on those pages (it used to switch the colour and nothing else). The editor's global column uses the same colour.
- **EQ ON / OFF** as the first card of EQ 1/2 (then LOW, MID, HIGH; EQ 2/2: LO HZ, MD HZ, Q, HI HZ). OFF hides the bands and runs no EQ code; switching off while a note sounds does not click. Every saved sound reads ON (its bands as they were).
- **DUCK SRC OFF.** A new sound's ducker starts at OFF and shows only SRC; PUMP, PART or MIDI bring their cards. The SIDECHAIN page lists each part's source.
- **The MOD MATRIX stays packed.** A slot whose SRC is set to OFF (on the unit or in the editor) lets the slots after it move up, in their order; a sound with gaps loads packed; the second matrix page is in the round only while slots 1..4 are all used. The sound does not change (the slots are summed in order).
- **An engine change keeps the sound.** Switching the engine (EDIT > ENGINE K1, or the editor's engine list) keeps the envelopes, filter, LFOs, matrix, FX, arp, mixer and the slot the sound came from; only the engine's own eight controls change (the ones the two engines share, such as CUT, RES, DTN, carry over; the rest start at their defaults), and switching back brings the engine's own eight back. INIT SOUND still resets everything to the engine's first sound.
- **COMP ATTACK and RELEASE in fine steps:** 40 a decade (0.1 to 100 ms, 20 ms to 2 s); the defaults 10 ms and 200 ms sound as before.
- **SEND FX says what it is:** under the cards, which effect is INACTIVE for the part (its send is 0) and TURN = JUMP TO SETTINGS.
- **Editor:** every bipolar control (PAN, the depths, KEY TRK, WIDTH, BALANCE-like ranges) draws its fill from the middle; the slider fill is the editor's own bar.
- **Tests:** the quick reset over every page and knob and under a live morph, PAN smoothing, EQ switch, packed matrix, engine change, every slider dragged in headless Chrome.
- **Owner edits into the factory set (for the developers; the owner's guide: [docs/OWNER_REFINE.md](docs/OWNER_REFINE.md)).** `felucca-base/tools/import_owner_edits.py` reads the Backup page's file and the PATCHES tab's patch file and writes `tools/owner_sounds.py` (the saved patches that differ from their factory sound, in the form of `tools/duell_sounds.py`, each replacing the factory sound of its slot) and `tools/owner_multis.py` with the saved multi records into `tools/factory_multis/` (kept byte for byte, marked `owner`). The build reads both (`gen_duell_sounds.py with_owner`, `factory_multis.py apply_owner`). The importer refuses with a reason what it cannot take (a slot holding another engine's sound, a name another sound has; an FM6 operator patch and SEQUENCER steps are left out, the values taken), merges with an earlier import, drops an entry that equals the recipe sound again (undo), and with `--audit` runs the sound audit and the multi check as a report; it never changes a value of his. The test `tests/owner_import_test.py` (`./host_tests.sh ownerimport`) saves every factory sound as the unit does (`tests/owner_export_gen.c`), imports the file and expects the factory data back unchanged; three turned knobs change exactly three sounds; a changed multi in a backup file is kept byte for byte.
- **Docs: "CPU on the unit"** (docs/TESTING.md): how to read the CPU bar and the TEST screen, why the unit reads higher than the host model on scenes heavy in PHYS, SHIM or the RANDOM delay (a count of the device code: the chip has no 64-bit multiply; the effect code runs from the flash through the cache; an estimate, not a measurement), where 0.2.23's M16 cost went by stage, and the safe polyphony. Also in KNOWN_ISSUES.

- **Editor round (the web editor).** Plain words everywhere, a reason wherever a control is hidden, UNDO, BYPASS, INIT, the FM6 voice editor, multis and patterns as files, the patch category.
  - **Every control spelled out, with a tooltip.** VOICES (POLY, MONO, LEGATO, UNISON), NOTE PRIORITY (LAST, LOWEST, HIGHEST), VOICE ALLOCATION (ROTATE, REUSE), GLIDE MODE, GLIDE, STEREO SPREAD, UNISON DETUNE, ENVELOPE SYNC, ATTACK / DECAY / SUSTAIN / RELEASE, PITCH / FILTER / SHAPE DEPTH, PATCH LEVEL (TRIM), the engine controls, the cards and tabs. One table to revise: `web/editor/labels.js`. The unit keeps its short labels.
  - **A hidden control says why** in its place (for example "NOTE PRIORITY: only with MONO, LEGATO or UNISON", "ATTACK: the operators have their own envelopes").
  - **VOICE: what is not heard is hidden.** NOTE PRIORITY needs MONO, LEGATO or UNISON; VOICE ALLOCATION needs POLY with GLIDE at 0; UNISON DETUNE needs UNISON; GLIDE MODE needs GLIDE above 0; STEREO SPREAD needs POLY or UNISON. The table, read from the firmware: docs/VOICE_CONTROLS.md.
  - **UNDO** of the last edits (a button per part, Ctrl+Z / Cmd+Z for the newest): a drag is one step, a card's reset is one step; loads and stores are not undone.
  - **Defaults** on every card: its controls back to their default values (one UNDO step).
  - **Init patch...** on each part: the engine's basic sound, as the unit's SAVE > TOOLS > INIT SOUND (or the ENGINE page's 4th knob). The mixer level, pan, mute, arpeggiator and pattern stay.
  - **BYPASS** on SATURATION, SENDS, INSERT, EQUALIZER, DUCKING (each part), CHORUS, DELAY, REVERB (both parts' sends) and the master stages, and **DRY (all effects off)** at the top of the GLOBAL column: it remembers the values and puts them back. Existing parameters only.
  - **The MASTER group**: COMPRESSOR, BASS MONO and STEREO WIDTH each in a card of their own, with what it does, ON / OFF, and the line that they are not used in DUEL.
  - **FM6 voice editor** (ENGINE tab of an FM6 part): the six operators (frequency ratio / fixed, coarse, fine, detune, output level, velocity and amp-modulation sensitivity, rate scaling, the envelope's four rates and levels with a picture, keyboard level scaling), the algorithm with a picture of how the operators connect, feedback, key sync, transpose, the pitch envelope and the LFO. An edit is heard at once; the part then plays its OWN voice (stored with the patch when you store it). VOICE SOURCE explained (factory voice F1..F11, OWN, voice bank B1..B32). Import of a DX7 .syx voice into the part, download of the voice as .syx.

It is in the list right after FRES. It works on every engine, also with the filter's CUT open (a MODE above LP runs the filter there). Stored as destination 46, after the others.


### Changed
- **The layout round (the owner's decisions on the layout proposal).**
  - **MIXER is the first page of the GLO button again:** K1 LEVEL 1, K2 PAN 1, K3 LEVEL 2, K4 PAN 2, both parts' cells and the level meters, the same as holding GLO. Each cell always reads MUTE and is lit in the part's colour while the part is muted (no LIVE / MUTED words); the mutes stay on OCT- / OCT+ with GLO held. HOME's MIXER page is gone (its SPACE knob was a copy of PERFORM's K2): HOME goes round MORPH, MORPH SET, MODE.
  - **STEREO MASTER** is one page for BASS MONO (K1) and WIDTH (K2), where the pages WIDTH and MASTER were. **COMPRESSOR** (was COMP): K1 RATIO with OFF as its lowest value (turning it up switches the compressor on), K2 THRESH, K3 ATTACK, K4 RELEASE.
  - **EQ** without its ON / OFF switch, each band's frequency before its gain: EQ 1/2 LOW FREQ, LOW GAIN, MID FREQ, MID GAIN; EQ 2/2 MID Q, HIGH FREQ, HIGH GAIN. A band at 0 dB costs nothing, as before.
  - **Pictures instead of the scope:** the EQ curve, the compressor's curve with a bar of what it takes off right now, the STEREO MASTER picture (mono below BASS MONO, the width above), the filter's response on both MULTI FILTER pages and the VCF, the arp's pattern on ARP 1/3 and 2/3, and the envelope or LFO curve on the DEST pages. UNISON DETUNE shows only in UNISON, SPREAD not in MONO or LEGATO.
  - **Editor:** each part column starts with its MIXER block (LEVEL, PAN, one MUTE button lit when muted), then a PATCH block (the slot and name, ENGINE, Patches..., Init patch..., PATCH LEVEL, the slot's category when the editor knows it); the GLOBAL tab's mixer card is gone. The EQUALIZER card has one BYPASS (the gains to 0 dB, brought back on the second press), hides its values while bypassed and draws the curve; STEREO MASTER and COMPRESSOR follow the unit, each with its picture. A long label breaks into two lines and its value stays beside it; no word twice on a card (SATURATION: DRIVE; SENDS: CHORUS, DELAY, REVERB; DELAY: MODE, TIME, MIX; DUCKING: SOURCE, DEPTH, ATTACK, RELEASE).
- **The part EQ costs about a third on the unit.** Its filter is rewritten for the unit's processor (32-bit multiplies instead of 64-bit ones): about 32 instructions a sample a band instead of about 85. It sounds the same, within 1 LSB of an ideal filter. A quiet tail now ends in exact silence.
- **Only the EQ bands you use run.** A band at 0 dB costs nothing; with only LOW set, only LOW runs. A band turned to 0 rings out over 23 ms instead of stopping at once.
- **SHIM's PITCH is UP or DOWN.** BOTH is gone (owner, see Removed).

The descriptions of the owner's edited multis M02 to M06 are deleted in the docs and the generator's data (names, parts and tempos stay).
- **Loading a multi sets its own mixer levels and its effects, and leaves the master alone.** A multi load sets each part's LEVEL, PAN, MUTE and sends and the effects the multi stores (chorus, the delay with its MODE, TIME, FDBK, COLR, SPREAD, MRATE / MDEPTH or CHANCE, the reverb with TYPE, SIZE, DAMP, LOCUT and the SHIMMER PITCH and MOD), whatever the levels were before. It never touched the MIDI settings, the clock source or TUNE, and it still does not. **BASS MONO is no longer part of a multi:** it is a master effect, a setting of your unit, so a load leaves it as it is and a save does not store it (a multi saved with 0.2.19 or 0.2.20 still loads; its BASS MONO is ignored). MULTI LOAD in the MENU (KEEPS MODE / SETS MODE) is unchanged.
- **The global effect pages are on the GLO button now.** CHORUS, DELAY 1/2, DELAY 2/2, REVERB, SHIMMER (still only with the reverb TYPE SHIM) and BASS MONO moved from FX to GLO (COMP, new, is next to BASS MONO), because they are the instrument's, not a part's. Their header now lights both part badges, like the other GLO pages.
  - **GLO, tap after tap (or SELECT):** TEMPO, CHORUS, DELAY 1/2, DELAY 2/2, REVERB, SHIMMER (with TYPE SHIM), WIDTH, MASTER, COMP, MODULES, SIDECHAIN, MIDI, MIDI LEARN. The first tap of GLO opens TEMPO. SIDECHAIN now sits with the MIDI pages, and MODULES moved before them.

- **Page order, signal order.** The engine's own filter page (VCF on ANALOG, TRIO FILTER, NOISE FILT) comes before the multimode filter, which runs after it. EDIT's round is ENGINE, EDIT 1, EDIT 2, the engine's own filter, MULTI FILTER 1/2, MULTI FILTER 2/2, VOICE 1/2, VOICE 2/2 (FM6: its OP pages after MULTI FILTER 2/2). The editor's ENGINE tab follows.
- **The CHORUS is a stereo chorus in STEREO and LAYER.** Before, the chorus was one moving delay in the middle of the sound: it thickened it but gave no width. Now it has two taps, the right one a quarter of an LFO turn behind the left, so the left and the right of the chorus move apart. RATE and DEPTH on the CHORUS page do what they did; there is no new knob, the width is fixed.
  - **Mono and the speaker:** the left channel of the chorus is exactly the old chorus, so the unit's built-in speaker (one channel) hears it as before, and the mono sum still sweeps. A quarter turn was chosen over half a turn because at half a turn the two taps mirror each other and the mono sum keeps almost no chorus (measured: the sweep of the mono sum is 30 to 50 dB at a quarter turn, 4 to 18 dB at half a turn, 53 to 66 dB for the old one-tap chorus).
  - **DUEL is unchanged:** each part has its own side there, so the chorus stays one tap on the part's own side, and the other channel stays exactly silent.
  - **Nothing else changes:** DEPTH 0, a chorus send of 0, the delay (PING-PONG, RANDOM, OCT UP and the delay modulation too), the reverbs, PERFORM, SPACE, SENDS OFF and STOP ALL are as before. A sound or multi that sends to the chorus in STEREO or LAYER sounds different (wider) and a little less loud in the middle.
  - **Cost:** about 46 host instructions per sample more, in STEREO and LAYER whenever the chorus bus runs (about 0.8 % of the CPU by the usual 1.7 % per 100 rule); none in DUEL. **While nothing reaches the chorus (its send is 0 or nothing plays) the plain delay bus leaves the chorus out** of the block, bit for bit the same result (a test compares both over silence, notes starting and stopping and DEPTH and RATE changes), so those mixes cost what they cost in 0.2.20. The delay's modulation, RANDOM and OCT UP always run it.
- **The delay's MODE works in DUEL.** Until now DUEL (part 1 left, part 2 right) always ran the plain delay and the MODE card was not shown. Now the card is there and every mode works, mono, on each part's own side:
  - **DIGI:** the plain delay, exactly as before.
  - **PING:** the same sound as DIGI. A ping-pong needs two sides to bounce between and a part has only one, so in DUEL PING is the plain delay (the card says PING). In STEREO and LAYER it is the ping-pong as before.
  - **RAND and OCT+:** the event delay, once for each part: on a random share of the beats the repeat is retriggered, played backwards or an octave up or down (RAND), or always an octave up (OCT+). The events stay in the middle of the part's side: no random pan, no width, so each part's delay stays in its own channel and the other channel stays silent. CHANCE is the share of beats, as in STEREO; MRATE is not shown in these two modes.
  - **SPREAD is not shown in DUEL** in any mode (there is nothing to spread).
- **A long delay time in DUEL.** Each part's delay has half of the delay line (0.74 s). With a delay time over about 0.35 s some events do not fit in it, a reverse, an octave down and a late repeat; on those beats the plain delay plays. An octave up fits up to about 0.65 s.
- **Nothing else changes.** The stored mode used to be kept but ignored in DUEL; now it applies. The factory multis store DIGI, so they sound the same, and STEREO and LAYER are bit for bit as before. Changing the mode crossfades in about 12 ms, and SENDS OFF and STOP ALL clear both parts' delays.
- **Cost:** in DUEL, RAND and OCT+ take about 110 to 125 host instructions per sample more for each part's delay while events play (about 56 for both parts between events, CHANCE 0); DIGI and PING cost what they did. The package is 2.6 KB larger and uses 76 bytes more of the pool.
- **The sidechain's MIDI setup is a setting of the unit, not of the sound.** GLO > SIDECHAIN now shows only the MIDI setup, for both parts (both badges): **K1 P1 CH, K2 P1 NOTE, K3 P2 CH, K4 P2 NOTE**: the MIDI channel (default 10) and the note (ANY or a note, default C2 = 36) that start the part's duck. They are saved with the unit's settings like the parts' MIDI channels: no patch, multi or project holds them, and loading one does not change them. They can be edited whatever the DUCK source is (PUMP too). OCT- and a knob puts that card back to its default. The rest of the ducker is the sound's and stays on FX > DUCK 1/2 and 2/2: the source is **PUMP, PART or MIDI** now (it was PUMP, PART, CH1 to CH16, with a NOTE card); with MIDI the second card of DUCK 1/2 is empty.
  - **Stored formats moved:** multi records are version 6, projects revision 4, the patch store's chunk version 2. Multis, projects and saved patches of the earlier formats are refused (before 1.0, as with every change of a stored meaning); the factory multis were rewritten.
  - **Editor:** a SIDECHAIN card on the MIDI tab of the GLOBAL column (the settings 38 to 41); the DUCK card of the FX tab keeps SRC, RATE or THR, DEPTH, ATK, RLS and CURVE.
  - **DUCK 1/2 and the SIDECHAIN page link to each other.** With SRC MIDI, DUCK 1/2 has a K4 card (SC MIDI, GLO, SETUP): turning it jumps to GLO > SIDECHAIN; nothing is edited on DUCK 1/2. The SIDECHAIN page shows THIS PATCH: PUMP (or PART, MIDI) for the selected part and says the setup is used only when a patch uses MIDI; the four settings stay editable whatever the SRC is.
- **KEY TRK starts at +51 % (owner: 50 %) in a new or initialised sound and in the factory sounds.** The step 32 of 63 (50 % falls between two steps). The factory BASS and PERC sounds and the kicks (35 sounds) are set to 0 on purpose, because with the filter's CUT open a KTRK above 0 closes the notes below C3. The other 93 factory sounds changed by it (the multimode filter now follows the keys in them; only the engines with the multimode filter hear it); their goldens were renewed and the sound audit stays at 0 FAIL. A saved patch stores KTRK against 0, so a stored value is never reinterpreted.

Up to 0.2.21 it was -50 to +50 cents. 440.0 Hz is exactly the sound of TUNE 0 before (every factory sound and multi renders the same). Turn it a step at a time, or fast: with KNOB ACCEL on, a fast turn moves several steps (up to 8 at a time), so the whole range takes about 64 quick clicks. It is still kept with the project and the autosave, as before. The pitch is within 0.21 cent of the exact frequency (a 0.1 Hz step at A4 is 0.39 cent).
  - **Editor:** the TUNE setting (MENU setting 22 and the global TUNE) shows Hz with one decimal. The protocol needed one new kind (2: a number in tenths); the 510 steps travel as one number.
- **Reverb LOW CUT goes up to 2 kHz, and starts at 299 Hz.** The knob still has OFF and 127 steps, now from **20 Hz to 2 kHz** (3.7 % a step; before, 20 to 450 Hz). Above 1000 Hz it shows kHz ("1.5 kHz"). The default is **299 Hz** (the step closest to 300 Hz), before 88 Hz, on a new unit, after ERASE DATA, with the reset gesture (OCT- and the knob), and in every factory multi. The slope stays 6 dB an octave. So the reverb tails of every factory multi and sound have less bass than before; nothing else about them changed (with LOCUT OFF all of them render exactly as in 0.2.20). A multi or project you saved keeps its stored step, which now means another frequency (step 61 was 88 Hz, it is 179 Hz).

### Fixed
- **The MASTER volume no longer glitches when it is turned (owner, 2026-10-20, in the headphones).** The volume used to change in steps, one step per block (0.73 ms) and per knob reading: small steps at the loud end, but up to 6 dB at the quiet end of the knob (the knob's law is a square, so one position of the pot is a large part of a small volume), and the quiet end also lost the last bits of the sound (the volume was applied to the 16-bit sound and cut back to 16 bits). Now the volume glides (about 12 ms), moves in finer steps at the quiet end, and keeps 7 more bits of the sound. Measured on a sine in the Mac copy: the unwanted sound beside the tone was 47 dB below it on a quick turn (now 74) and 27 to 40 dB below it at the quiet end (now 66 to 77). Cost about 18 host instructions a sample (0.5 % of the time budget), 31 while the knob moves.

- **PHASE: the high whistle in the attack of a sound whose envelope opens DCW (#31 BRAZEN, owner 2026-10-20) is gone.** PHASE builds its bent wave once a block, so while the envelope sweeps DCW (the amp envelope's ENV amount, and a little with every PHASE sound) the wave stepped at each block seam: a buzz at 1,378 Hz and its harmonics, 62 to 77 dB under full scale above 3 kHz, exactly absent with ENV 0 and less audible at BRAZEN's morph end B, whose own highs cover it. The step is now carried across the seam as a short glide: 10 to 15 dB less on middle notes. Aliasing of the bent wave is not the cause (84 dB under at C4 with DCW 116; 56 dB at C6) and stays: a bit of it is normal for phase distortion. Costs about 13 host instructions a sample (+6 %) for the dearest PHASE voice while DCW moves a lot, nothing while it stands.

- **The EQ's HIGH knob did nothing at the default Q.** The HIGH band only ran when MID's Q was turned away from its middle. Now it runs whenever its gain is set. 18 factory sounds store a HIGH gain (most of the basses at -4 dB) and now sound with it: #2 FOLD BASS, #4 RUBBER BAND, #7 FM BASS 2, #8 FRETLESS, #9 SLAP DOCTOR, #10 ELASTIC BASS, #11 FUZZ BASS, #12 ARP BASS, #13 CARTRIDGE, #14 BULLFROG, #15 BREADBIN, #16 SID BASS, #17 SYNC BASS, #18 PEDAL BASS, #19 PLUCK BASS, #20 GEARBOX, #23 KICK BASS, #95 DEEP KICK (-2 dB); so do the multis M02, M03, M08, M13 and M16.
- **The EQ could keep a small DC offset** on a LOW band at its lowest frequencies until the part went silent. Gone.

- **WAVE: SUB and DTN no longer crackle when you turn them on a held note.** SUB's level used to jump at every knob step, and DTN switched its second oscillator in or out at once when it left or reached 0. Both were clicks, clearest on a pure sine (an EMPTY user table plays one). SUB now glides to the knob (90 % in about 6 ms) and DTN fades its second oscillator in or out over 5.8 ms. A value that does not move sounds exactly as before, and so does the start of a note. Matrix routes on SUB or DTN (an LFO breathing the sub, an LFO sweeping DTN through 0 on #13 HOOVER) are smooth now too.
- **Multimode filter: no click at the open end.** CUT 82 (fully open) bypasses the filter, and crossing between 81 and 82 on a sounding note used to click (about 4 times the music's sharpest corner on #83 TINE ROOM, which sits at 82). Crossing the edge, by the knob, an envelope, an LFO or KEY TRK, is now cross-faded over 0.7 ms. Sounds whose cutoff never crosses the edge are unchanged.
- **An EMPTY user wavetable (USR1..USR3 unset) plays a clean sine.** That was already so and is now tested; the "That slot is empty" message comes from the editor, not from the unit.
- **LAYER: each part uses its own TRN from the keys, and the SPLIT compares the key you play.** Before, part 2 was given part 1's already transposed note, so part 2's TRN did nothing from the keys (with the arp on it did), and the split was compared with part 1's transposed note: a split shown as F3 with part 1 at TRN -12 sat at F4. Now the split is the key as played, whatever the TRN, and every part adds its own TRN. In the factory multis: M02 NIGHT ORGAN and M03 PAD SPLIT now say split F4 (they sound the same split as in the test build); in M03 the pad now sounds at the key (it played an octave lower before, with part 1's TRN -12); in M08 FINAL REEL the strings' TRN +12 now sounds an octave up from the keys, as it was made in 0.2.11. The arp-driven parts (M02's organ) are unchanged. No render of the sound audit or the multi goldens changes.
- **The editor's Backup page read a multi of this firmware as damaged.** Its content check still expected multi records of version 2 with 119 values, so a backup holding a saved multi (version 6, 127 values) was refused ("damaged multi") on reading and restoring. It now checks version 6 and 127 values, the form the unit writes and checks itself. Found while preparing the owner's edit path above (the multis come from the Backup file). Since the editor round an object the editor cannot check no longer stops the backup: it is kept in the file as it was read, with a warning naming it (for example "Object 13 (multis M01 and M02) ..."), and a restore skips it unless you tick the box.
- **PAN crackled** when turned from the editor (and would have from the new knob): the pan gain is followed smoothly now (a knob step lands within about 13 ms, the plain mix ramps within the block; the spread mix steps once a block). Every render of before is bit for bit the same while the pan does not move.
- **SAT crackled** when it was changed on a sounding part (heard on an FM part): the drive, its tone and its wet/dry blend stepped at the block boundary. The part's SAT is followed smoothly now (a quarter of what is left each block, at most one and a half steps a block; a knob step lands within about 10 ms, the whole range within about 140 ms; a silent part takes it at once). A SAT that does not move is bit for bit as before. `./host_tests.sh sat` measures the step at the block boundaries on ANALOG, PHASE and FM6.
- **SAT modulated from the matrix no longer clicks when the LFO restarts.** With a matrix route LFO 2 (or LFO 1, an envelope) to SAT and the LFO on TRIG NOTE, a new note restarted the LFO and the SAT jumped at once (up to its whole depth) while earlier notes still sounded. The SAT the part runs at, the stored value plus the matrix, now goes through the same smoothing as the knob: a jump ramps over a few milliseconds (at most 1.5 steps a block). A SAT that does not move is bit for bit as before; an LFO of 1.1 Hz running on its own (not restarted by notes) at depth 98 is followed within 2 steps of 127. Test: tests/sat_test.c (RETRIG, SLOW).
- **The HALL reverb's tail no longer stops at once, like a noise gate** (heard on M07 FINAL REEL and on released pads). ## DUELL 0.2.20 beta

Four additions to the firmware (BASS MONO, a pitch and drift for the SHIM reverb, the patch store's PATCH_RAW command, and a mixer level apart from the sound's trim) and three to the web editor (a patch library with DX7 import and wavetable files, and a piano roll).

### Added
- **BASS MONO (a page of the GLO button, KNOB 1).** A sound setting, so it is not in the MENU. The page, after SIDECHAIN, is the master-stage page: it is called BASS MONO for now and will be MASTER when more master controls come to it. It is global, one setting for the instrument, and it is saved with each multi and with the project. Makes the output mono below a frequency you choose and keeps it stereo above it, so wide effects (SPACE, chorus, SPREAD, PING-PONG, the reverbs) do not smear the low end. It helps in clubs and when cutting vinyl.
  - **Values:** OFF (the default), then 40, 50, 63, 80, 100, 125, 160, 200, 250, 315 or 400 Hz. 100 to 150 Hz is the usual club setting. A multi saved with 100 Hz brings it back when it is loaded; the factory multis are OFF. In DUEL mode the card is not shown and the stage does not run.
  - **How it works:** the left-minus-right part of the sound is high-passed at that frequency (12 dB an octave, 3 dB down at the printed frequency); the middle is not touched. At 100 Hz a 40 Hz out-of-phase tone is 16 dB down; at 160 Hz, 24 dB down; a 2 kHz out-of-phase tone passes unchanged.
  - **Where in the signal path:** after the effects, SPACE and PERFORM, before MASTER and the limiter. USB audio gets it too (it is the same output).
  - **STEREO and LAYER only.** In DUEL (part 1 left, part 2 right for an external mixer) it would mix the two parts, so it does not run there and its card is not shown. The value is kept for when you go back to STEREO or LAYER.
  - **OFF is exactly the sound as before** (bit for bit). Switching it on or off, or changing the frequency, fades over about 25 ms: no click.
  - **Editor:** SETTINGS > AUDIO has the same setting (id 33). The editor cannot see the mode, so it keeps showing it, with a note that the unit ignores it in DUEL.
  - **Cost:** about 39 host instructions per sample while it is on (about 0.7 % of the device CPU, by the usual 1.7 % per 100 rule), nothing measurable when it is off. The fast RAM block is unchanged.

## DUELL 0.2.16 beta

### Added
- **WAVE user tables: three slots of your own, USR1 to USR3 (TABLE 26 to 28).** After the 26 factory tables the TABLE knob has three more places. A user table is made on the computer from a WAV file and sent to the unit by the editor; the unit keeps it (flash, with a CRC) and plays it like any table.
  - **What a user table is:** 19 frames of 64 harmonics, one byte each (a level in 0.38 dB steps, the frame's strongest harmonic is the top). Every harmonic is a sine, the same in all frames, as the factory tables are, so POS mixes two frames as a mix of their spectra. **Phases are not kept, and a file with more frames or more harmonics is reduced to 19 and 64: the editor says so.** A Serum-style file (256 frames of 2048 samples) keeps 19 evenly spaced frames and loses the harmonics above 64 (for a sharp saw that is about 0.9 % of the energy, 20 dB down, the bright edge) and the phases.
  - **Name:** up to 8 characters, set when you send it; TABLE shows it (a card shows the first five). A slot with no table is named **EMPTY** and plays a plain **sine** in every frame. A patch, multi or save names a user table by its number only; the table stays in the slot. On a unit where that slot is empty, or holds another table, the patch plays the sine, or the other table.
  - **Writing is only ever done by the editor, never while the sequencer plays.** The unit stops the sequencer first and refuses (rc 3) if it does not stop; the flash write ducks the audio like any save. A part that is playing the slot fades out and builds the new table (a moment of silence, like a TABLE change). A power cut during a write leaves the old table.
  - **Where they live:** flash 0xA0000 to 0xA5FFF (USR1 0xA0000 and 0xA1000, USR2 0xA2000 and 0xA3000, USR3 0xA4000 and 0xA5000: an A/B pair of 4 KiB sectors each, 1228 bytes of table). This is the first 24 KiB of the old user sample region; the patch store takes the next 80 KiB (0xA6000 to 0xB9FFF, see Changed) and 0xBA000 to 0xC7FFF (56 KiB) stays free. A firmware update never touches it. ERASE DATA erases the three tables too (they are your data).
  - **Backup:** the editor's backup holds the three tables (object ids 24 to 26, INFO backup caps bit 3); a backup file with them is "version 3".
  - **Editor protocol:** WT_LIST, WT_GET and WT_PUT (commands 81 to 83), see EDITOR_PROTOCOL.
  - **The converter** (`web/editor/wavetable.js`, with a try-out page `web/editor/wavetable_demo.html` that talks to the editor's simulated unit): reads PCM and float WAV files, frames of 256 to 4096 samples (or any size of 130 and up: a 600 sample cycle works), a whole file of 2048-sample frames (the clm chunk of Serum, Vital and Surge names the size), picks 19 frames evenly, takes the first 64 harmonics with an FFT, scales each frame, and shows the spectrum and what is lost. The editor page for it is not built yet.
  - Credits: the FFT (Cooley and Tukey, 1965) and the 2048-sample wavetable file convention (Xfer Serum, also Vital and Surge) are credited in NOTICE.md, LICENSING.md and MENU > ABOUT > CREDITS. No code of other projects is used.
- **CLK AUTO: the clock finds itself, and it is the new default.** GLO > TEMPO, KNOB 4 CLK now offers AUTO, INT, USB, TRS. On AUTO, DUELL plays on its own tempo until a MIDI clock arrives on USB or on the TRS MIDI input; then it follows that clock. When the clock stops for about a second, DUELL goes back to its own tempo.
  - **Nothing stops when it switches.** The pattern, the arp, the synced LFOs, envelopes, delay and PUMP keep running. When the clock goes away, DUELL carries on at the last tempo and in time, with no gap.
  - It takes three clock ticks to switch (a single stray tick is ignored), or a Start / Continue from that input, which switches at once.
  - While it follows one input, the other input's clock and Start / Stop are ignored.
  - Start, Stop and Continue from the followed input work as with CLK USB or TRS.
  - The CLK card shows AUTO with the clock in use under it (INT, USB or TRS). The TEMPO card shows EXT with USB or TRS under it while it follows a clock. Each switch says CLOCK USB, CLOCK TRS or CLOCK INT in the header.
  - INT, USB and TRS work as before.
- **GLO > MODULES: switch the arp, the sequencer, the send effects and the part effects off for the whole unit.** A new page after MIDI (GLO, TEMPO, SIDECHAIN, MIDI, MODULES, MIDI LEARN) with four switches, each ON (the default) or OFF: K1 ARP, K2 SEQ, K3 SENDS, K4 PART FX. Turn a knob right for ON, left for OFF.
  - **ARP OFF:** no arp plays, whatever its MODE says; the keys play directly (chord keys still work). HOLD ALL still latches the keys; HOLD ARP latches nothing while the arp is off. REC (as HOLD) switches between OFF and ALL.
  - **SEQ OFF:** the step sequencer plays no stored steps: PLAY and a MIDI Start run the tempo, the arps and the synced LFOs, but no step, lock or automation. The arp's SEQUENCER mode is off too (it plays the same steps). You can still edit and record steps, and STEP PREVIEW still sounds them.
  - **SENDS OFF:** the chorus, delay and reverb (and SPACE) are switched off after a short fade, and their processing stops: this saves a lot of CPU (about 9 to 21 % on the factory multis). Switched on again, they start clean, with no old echo.
  - **PART FX OFF:** each part's SAT, INSERT, EQ and DUCK are bypassed after a short fade (up to about 11 % CPU saved when they are used).
  - These are device settings: kept when you power off, not saved in patches or multis, and a multi load never changes them. A new unit, ERASE DATA and the update start with all four ON. Nothing you set on the ARP, SEQ or FX pages is changed: switch a module ON and it plays as before.
  - While a module is OFF, the cards it affects are greyed with a short reason (ARP OFF, SEQ OFF, SND OFF, FX OFF) and the footer says it in full, for example MODULES: SENDS OFF. You can still turn them; the value is kept for later. PERFORM's effects are never switched off.
  - OCT- held + a knob switches that module back ON. The web editor shows them in a MODULES group.
- **PLATE, a sixth reverb.** REVERB > TYPE now has ROOM, SPRING, HALL, SHIM, RESO and PLATE: a smooth, dense plate reverb in stereo, with a slow gentle movement in the tail. SIZE is how long the tail lasts (about 0.5 s at 0, 3 s at 90, 6 s at 127), DAMP how dark it gets as it fades. It works in STEREO, LAYER and DUEL (each part's own reverb on its own side), with the SENDS switch and with SPACE, and you can change to it and away from it while a tail rings without a click. It uses no extra memory and about 1.3 times the CPU of ROOM (a little more than HALL). Based on the reverb of the CHOMPI firmware (MIT), which is Emilie Gillet's plate reverb from Mutable Instruments Rings and Clouds (MIT); the licence notices are in LICENSES/MIT-CHOMPI.txt and NOTICE.md.
- **Two new delay modes: RANDOM and OCT UP bend the repeats on the beat.** The delay now has a MODE, the first card of DELAY 1/2: DIGITAL (the delay as it always was), PING-PONG (the repeats alternate left and right, as the old PING switch did), RANDOM and OCT UP. The cards read DIGI, PING, RAND and OCT+; the header says the full name when you turn MODE.
  - **RANDOM:** on a random share of the clock edges the repeat is retriggered a little late, played backwards, an octave up or an octave down, for one beat; then the plain delay comes back. **OCT UP:** the same chances, but always an octave up, with a random place in the stereo image; fed back, the octave-up repeats climb, and COLR (the delay's tone) tames them.
  - The clock is the delay's own TIME: one edge per delay time (1/8 note: an edge every 1/8 note), counted from PLAY and following the tempo and an external clock like the delay does. On DELAY 2/2, DEPTH becomes **CHANCE** (0 to 100 %: the share of the edges that fire an event; it is the same stored value as DEPTH, so a DEPTH of 40 is a CHANCE of 40). The matrix destination for DEPTH reads CHNC in these two modes. RATE (the delay's slow modulation) does not exist in them and fades with the mode's name as the reason. SPREAD is the width of the repeats, FDBK, COLR and MIX are the delay's as before.
  - **CHANCE starts at 40 %.** Turning MODE into RANDOM or OCT UP while the stored DEPTH / CHANCE is 0 sets it to 40 % and the header says CHANCE 40 %, so the mode does something at once (the CHOMPI original starts its randomness knob at 0, so there is no number to copy; its events fire on at most half of the edges, DUELL's 100 % is every edge). Turning MODE back to DIGITAL or PING-PONG puts that 40 back to 0 if you did not touch the knob meanwhile, so the delay sounds as it did; a DEPTH that was not 0 is left as it is, and a multi that stores RANDOM with CHANCE 0 keeps its 0. The web editor does the same when you change MODE.
  - **Stereo:** RANDOM and OCT UP are not a second ping-pong: the repeats do not alternate left and right. The left channel reads the delay line, the right one 10 ms behind it (a small width), the events sit in the middle (RANDOM) or at a random pan of up to 60 % to either side (OCT UP), and SPREAD sets the width. The alternating left/right echo is the mode PING-PONG.
  - PING-PONG, RANDOM and OCT UP are STEREO and LAYER only: in DUEL (each part its own delay) the plain delay runs and the MODE card fades with DUEL. The delay time of these modes is at most 0.74 s (a longer TIME halves, as it did for PING-PONG).
  - Changing the mode while the delay rings crossfades in about 12 ms: no click. SENDS OFF and STOP ALL clear it like the rest of the delay. It uses no extra memory for the sound (it reads the delay's own line), 76 bytes of state, and costs about 45 more host instructions per sample than the plain delay between events (about 1 % CPU) and about 130 to 165 when events sound most of the time (CHANCE 50 to 100: about 2 to 3 %), 100 to 130 more than PING-PONG. About 3.3 KB of flash. No lock yet.
  - Based on the idea of the granular delay in the TEMPO firmware of CHOMPI (CHOMPI Club, MIT); written again here in integer code, no code copied.
- **REVERB LOW CUT: no reverb makes the bass muddy.** The REVERB page has a fourth card, K4 LOCUT: a gentle high-pass (6 dB an octave) on what goes into the reverb, in front of every model: ROOM, SPRING, HALL, SHIM, RESO and PLATE alike. It shows its corner in Hz, from 20 Hz to 450 Hz on an even-step scale (each step about 2.5 % higher), and OFF at 0. It starts at 88 Hz (value 61), a mild cut: low notes keep their body in the dry sound, but they no longer fill the reverb with boom. Only the reverb's input is filtered; the dry sound, the chorus and the delay are not. It is one setting for the whole unit, kept in saves and multis like SIZE and DAMP; in DUEL each part's reverb has its own filter. It works with SPACE (SPACE does not bypass it), with MODULES SENDS and with PERFORM. Turning it is smooth, no click or zipper noise. It also removes any steady offset from what the reverb gets, which PLATE needed. At OFF the reverb sounds exactly as before. It costs about 0.16 % CPU a bus.

The biggest possible saved sound (an FM6 sound with its patch, a SEQ-mode arp with 16 steps, End B and every value off its default) is 435 bytes; 8 of them fit a chunk, and the store now has 16 chunks, so 16 x 8 = 128 of them fit: a save never fails half way, and no message about size or room exists (a slot is never "full"). Typical sounds are 55 to 90 bytes (an FM6 sound about 170 to 190), so the 16 chunks are mostly empty room. The 128 slots are one list: a slot holds the sound you saved there, or else the factory sound at that place of the list (0.2.17 above).
  - **Where:** ten new chunks (ten more A/B pairs of 4 KiB sectors, 80 KiB) at 0xA6000 to 0xB9FFF, next to the six old ones. A payload ends at sector offset 0xF00 as before (the updater scans the last 256 bytes of a sector; a compile-time check and a test make sure). **0xBA000 to 0xC7FFF (56 KiB, 14 sectors) stays free** for later. The old store is read as it is, nothing is wiped. A firmware update keeps it; ERASE DATA erases all sixteen chunks.
  - **RAM: 14.8 KB of the pool back.** The patch store no longer keeps a copy of its chunks in RAM (23 KB). It keeps a small list (128 entries: where each saved sound is, its name, engine and category) and reads a saved sound from flash when it is loaded or edited. Pool spare 14,140 to 28,944 bytes; the app is 427,948 bytes (was 427,444); the fast RAM block is unchanged (12,312 bytes, 200 free). (0.2.17: see its numbers above.)
  - **The PATCHES page:** the list is the 128 slots; the cards show the slot's number and no count of used or free places.
  - **Program change:** program n is slot n, 0..127.
  - **Editor:** the patch page is the grid of the 128 slots; it shows no "N of 128 used" and reads the slot count from the unit (INFO tag 58 01).
- **The firmware is 29 KB smaller and uses 1.4 KB less RAM.** The app is 421,408 bytes (it was 450,020; the limit is 581,564), so 160,156 bytes are free for new features. Nothing you can use changed; see Removed.
- **PERFORM lets go of SPACE and KILL when you leave it.** Before, leaving PERFORM reset KILL only, and SPACE stayed where you left it, even after you loaded another patch ("still in immense space"). Now, by default, when you leave PERFORM, load a patch or load a multi, SPACE and KILL glide back to neutral in under half a second (0.4 s from the ends of the knob, no click, no jump): SPACE to the patch's own sends, KILL to OFF, and then the sound is exactly the patch's own again. A SPACE you set on the MIXER page alone is left alone when you only open and leave PERFORM. DEPTH and MORPH stay as they are.
  - **MENU > CONTROL > PERF LEAVE: FADE (default) / KEEP.** KEEP leaves SPACE and KILL as they are after you leave PERFORM and after any load, and the cards keep showing the live values.
- **In PERFORM the black keys play in the scale.** They still play notes, now moved to the nearest note of the SCALE and ROOT you set, even if OFF-SCALE says PLAY (CHROMATIC: unchanged). Outside PERFORM nothing changes.
- **The multimode filter's page is called MULTI FILTER, and the DIST knob is called SAT.** On ANALOG the engine's own filter page is VCF and the multimode one was just FILTER; now EDIT > MULTI FILTER (its hold layer: QUICK MULTI) is clearly not the engine's own filter. TRIO FILTER and NOISE FILT stay as they are. The FX page's first knob, the part's DIST, is not much of a distortion (it is gentle), so it is now SAT (saturation): the card, the matrix destination (DEST SAT, SATURATION on its line), the MORPH and editor labels and the PART FX text on MODULES. Nothing stored changes: sounds, matrix routes and the editor's ids are the same. PERFORM's K3 FILTER (the DJ filter) keeps its name.
- **PERFORM's K3 FILTER is now KILL, and the matrix destinations say MULTI FILTER.** K3 is the DJ-style filter that goes to silence at both ends (the kill zone), so it is named for what it does and no longer shares the name FILTER with the EDIT pages; its reset says RESET KILL. In the matrix, the destinations FILTER CUTOFF and FILTER RESONANCE are now MULTI FILTER CUTOFF and MULTI FILTER RESONANCE on the DEST line (the stored numbers are the same).
- **The MENU is tidier.** Hold HOME for it. Its rows, tab by tab:
  - **DISPLAY:** COLOR, LEDS, SCALE LEDS (moved here from CONTROL), ANIMATION, SCREEN OFF, CPU METER.
  - **CONTROL:** MULTI LOAD, HOME OPENS, KNOB ACCEL, BUTTON HOLD, STEP KEYS, STEP PREVIEW, PERF LEAVE.
  - **AUDIO:** OUTPUT EQ, USB LEVEL, CLICK, CLICK LEVEL, COUNT-IN, TUNE.
  - **SYSTEM:** RESTORE LAST, BUTTON MAP, USB SERIAL, GUIDE, ABOUT, ERASE DATA.
- **New names, same jobs.** ANIM is now ANIMATION. HOLD is BUTTON HOLD (how long you hold a button for its quick layer). SPEAKER EQ is OUTPUT EQ (it acts on every output, not only the speaker). MODE RECALL is MULTI LOAD: KEEPS MODE (the default) or SETS MODE (a multi load also sets MODE, BALANCE, XFADE and SPLIT). CHORD ENTRY is STEP KEYS: WRITE or ADD. CALIBRATION is BUTTON MAP (it teaches the firmware which button and knob is which; you never need it in normal use).
- **USB SERIAL shows a second line, DEVELOPER CONSOLE**, so you know what it is.
- **A tab with more rows than fit on the screen gets a second page**, shown as SYSTEM 1/2 in its tab. SELECT goes through every row and every page as before.
- **New defaults:** KNOB ACCEL is ON (fast turns move big values faster), CLICK is REC (the metronome clicks while you record on the sequencer pages), USB SERIAL is OFF.
  - A new unit and ERASE DATA start with them.
  - **After the update, once:** KNOB ACCEL is set ON, CLICK OFF becomes REC and USB SERIAL ON becomes OFF, because the firmware cannot tell whether you chose the old value or never touched it. If you had set them that way on purpose, set them again in the MENU: from then on your choice stays.
  - With USB SERIAL ON, macOS 13 to 15 do not show the USB audio input; OFF fixes that. Turn it ON only if you use the serial console.
- **QUICK MULTI (hold EDIT) is the multimode filter on every engine.** K1 CUT, K2 RES, K3 MODE of the multimode filter (the MULTI FILTER page's), K4 the amp envelope's REL, the same on all ten engines. On ANALOG, TRIO and NOISE it used to show the engine's own filter instead (ANALOG: CUT, RES, TYPE, so K3 switched the VCF between LP and BP). Their own filters are still on their EDIT pages: VCF, TRIO FILTER, NOISE FILT. SLOPE stays on the MULTI FILTER page.
- **The MIXER page shows both parts side by side.** Part 1 on the left, part 2 on the right, like the GLO page: each side has the part's number and sound, LEVEL in dB, PAN (in DUEL fixed: LEFT / RIGHT, greyed), the REV, DLY and CHO sends and a MUTE mark when muted, with the two level meters of the GLO page (peak hold, CLIP) between them. The knobs still edit the selected part (LEVEL, PAN, SPACE, MUTE); its side has the bright outline in its colour, and ALGORITHM switches the part. Before, the two parts were two strips one above the other.
- **CRUSH BITS works over the whole knob.** Before, 16 to 9 bits took half of the knob and sounded alike, so the first clicks from 16 changed nothing. Now every detent is a different depth, and the number on the card follows it (the nearest whole bit count):
  - 1 bit at the left end, 10 bits at about 85 % of the knob, then 11 to 16 bits in the last 18 detents, 16 (clean) only on the last two.
  - Between the whole bit counts the depth is in-between, so there are no dead clicks. The card shows the whole number; 16 is shown only where the sound is really clean.
  - **Your saved sounds keep their sound.** Patches, multis, saved work and the factory multis load with CRUSH on the same bit count they had (checked bit for bit). The factory sound GLASS SHARDS is still 8 bits. A new CRUSH on a part with the knob untouched starts at about 6 bits (the knob stays where it was; the old default was 9 bits). The default for a reset of the BITS knob (OCT- and the knob) is 9 bits.
- **USB name: the unit shows up as DUELL, made by OSCILL/R.** It was FELUCCA by Hügelton Instruments. Same USB vendor and product numbers (0x1209 / 0x0001). The installer and the web editor find it by either name. The update loader keeps its own name.
- **The hottest audio code runs from the chip's RAM** instead of the flash (the filter, FM6 operators, WAVE, the effects chain). The sound is the same; only where the code runs changes. Every build is checked after linking (tools/elfcheck.py).
- **Two new INSERT types, TAPE and VINYL, at the end of the TYPE list.** They share the INSERT's MIX (0 is the part untouched, bit for bit) and the same fade when you change TYPE or MIX. Each part has its own.
  - **TAPE:** WOW (how far the pitch drifts, slowly and at random, 0.5 to 2 Hz; 0 holds it still), SAT (a soft saturation, 1x to 5x, the level kept about the same), TONE (a gentle high cut). A flutter of 6 to 9 Hz (about 3 cents) and a faint hiss are always part of TAPE.
  - **VINYL:** DUST (the crackle, clicks of random size and sign a second: 0 none, about 1 at the left end, about 16 in the middle, about 250 at the right end), NOISE (a quiet bed of high-passed noise; 0 none), WARP (a light wobble of the pitch once a turn of a 33 rpm record, 0.56 Hz; 0 holds it still). The sound is always narrowed a little (below about 110 Hz and above about 6 kHz); the crackle and the noise are added after that.
  - The hiss, the dust and the crackle are there while the part sounds (and for a moment after); the INSERT does not run on a silent part. The same part and the same settings always give the same noise.
  - Each costs about as much as PHASR (about 2.8 % of the device's half per part with a 4-note part, measured on the host model; not yet on a unit).

### Fixed
- **Installer: the backup works with this firmware.** The installer's "Save a backup file" refused DUELL 0.2.14 and later ("This firmware cannot make a complete backup"): it expected the 16 song rows the old Felucca sent, and DUELL sends none, and it did not know the longer list of objects (30 with the user wavetables, 40 with the 16 patch chunks of 0.2.15, whose ids 35 to 39 it also took for sample slots). It now reads the unit's INFO tags, takes the list the unit sends (16, 27, 30 or 40 objects) and saves them all: patches (all 16 chunks), multis, pattern slots, wavetables, settings and the music. The file is the same "felucca-backup" file as before; the editor's Backup page restores it, and a file from an older unit still restores onto a new one (the chunks it lacks are emptied) and a new file onto an older unit (what that unit does not know is skipped). The note under the button now tells the truth about what is in the file.
- **STOP ALL now silences the effect tails too.** Pressing PLAY/STOP twice (or holding it) used to stop the voices but let the delay, chorus and reverb ring on, for more than 30 s with SPACE at +100. Now their lines fade out in about 25 ms, are cleared, and the sends fade back in about 22 ms later, so the next notes sound as normal. It works in LAYER, DUEL, STEREO and SPLIT with every reverb type. A double press on a stopped unit does it too. A single STOP is unchanged (the tails run on), and GLO > MODULES SENDS OFF stays OFF.
- **ELASTIC BASS (#8): its MORPH End B now does something.** End B was too close to the sound; its amount is 11 % and it was rolled again: B is rounder and deeper (more at 100 to 200 Hz, a little less top), still the same acid bass.
- The keys and buttons no longer flash bright for a moment when DUELL saves; they go dark for that moment instead.
- USB: the audio input is listed before MIDI so macOS 15 and older show it; computers may treat the unit as a new device once.
- **PERFORM K3 KILL (was FILTER): no more noise or crackle.** Turned from the middle it added noise, and deep into the low-pass it crackled (the old filter ran out of precision at low cutoffs). It is now a new 24 dB/oct stereo filter: low-pass to the left, high-pass to the right, still exactly off in the middle and silent at -100 / +100. Clean at every setting, a smooth sweep while you turn, a steeper slope.
- **SAT: the first detents no longer jump.** SAT 1 used to cut the bass and the level at once, so the step from OFF was audible. Now SAT 1 to 11 mixes the clean sound with the saturated one (1/12 of the effect at 1, 11/12 at 11); from 12 up it is the same sound as before. SAT 0 is still the plain, untouched sound. Switching SAT on from 0 also starts its filters fresh, so it no longer carries leftovers from the last time it was on.
  - Factory sounds with a small SAT, or with a small SAT in their End B, now sound a little cleaner at the low end. ELASTIC BASS and WALL OF SAW got a smaller MORPH AMOUNT (6 % and 15 %, was 7 % and 17 %) so their End B stays as loud as the other sounds'.
- **FM6: amplitude modulation sensitivity (AMS) was broken.** Any 6-operator voice with AMS above 0 on a sounding operator was scrambled (noise, mutes, or up to four times full level), even with the LFO's AMD at 0, which is how many DX7 banks are set. AMS now follows the DX7: AMD 0 changes nothing, and AMD raises the dip with AMS 1 to 3. No factory sound uses AMS, so none of them changes. Found by running 384 voices from 12 public DX7 banks through the engine: 7 voices clipped and 19 were noise-like before, none clip and 3 are noise-like now (those 3 are noisy in the voice data too).
- **Editor: one corrupt voice no longer drops the rest of a DX7 bank.** A voice with bytes above 0x7F (not valid MIDI data) used to end the import there and lose every voice after it. Now only that voice is skipped, and the editor names it ("corrupt voice skipped: #31 Klick"); the others are kept.

  - **The DIGITAL engine's conversion to FM6.** DIGITAL has been gone since 1.0; the code that turned old DIGITAL sounds into FM6 sounds is removed too.
  - **The OVERVIEW screen's drawing code,** which was still in the firmware after its page was taken out of HOME's round, with its scope, TRACKS and LEVELS views.
  - A few icons and one brightness level of the key LED scan that only these used. The ABOUT credits no longer name the DRUM voices.
- **Loading of saves from before 0.2.14.** The unit now loads only what 0.2.14 itself writes. Older saved work, multis, patches and settings files are not read any more, and nothing is converted: the code for the old formats is gone (about 4.7 KB of firmware). This is a test beta before 1.0, so there is no conversion.
  - **Saved work (the working state the unit restores at power-on, and the web editor's backup of it) from 0.2.13 and before:** ignored. After the update the unit starts with its power-on sounds and CLK AUTO, and saves its own state from then on. - **The OVERVIEW page.** HOME now goes round MORPH, MORPH SET, MODE and MIXER, then MORPH again. HOME OPENS offers MORPH, MODE and MIXER (if it was set to OVERVIEW, HOME opens MORPH).
- **MENU > DISPLAY > SCOPE and OVERVIEW.** The scope always follows the volume knob, as it did by default.

Editors: INS A of CRUSH changed meaning, see EDITOR_PROTOCOL.
- Your settings stay, apart from the three new defaults above (once).
- Editor: the MENU settings keep their numbers; SCOPE (18) and OVERVIEW (23) are retired and no longer listed; PERF LEAVE is the new id 32 (FADE / KEEP, tab CONTROL), 25 items in all with GLO > MODULES' four (ids 28..31). Older editors show the new names, which come from the device.

- Computers may show the unit as a new MIDI / audio device once, now named DUELL (the MIDI port names changed).

The PERFORM page shows the keys and four knobs: MORPH, SPACE (the delay, reverb and chorus smaller to the left, huge to the right), FILTER (low-pass left, high-pass right, OFF in the middle) and DEPTH (how strong the effects are); there PRESETS is BALANCE and ALGORITHM picks PART 1, PART 2 or BOTH. A tap of FX goes back. Nothing about it is saved, and with no key held and the knobs in the middle the sound is exactly as before. A tap of FX still opens the FX pages.
- **Scale lights are automatic.** When the global SCALE is not CHROMATIC, the keys now show it by themselves: the scale's keys are lit about half bright, the root keys fully lit, the keys outside the scale dark (or, with OFF-SCALE KEYS on PLAY, a faint glow), and the key you press or the note that sounds lights fully. No menu step is needed; MENU > CONTROL > SCALE LEDS is now AUTO (default) or OFF. With CHROMATIC the keys look as before. Saved settings load as AUTO.
- **Delay modulation.** DELAY 2/2 has two new knobs, DEPTH and RATE: the delay gets its own small LFO that gently wobbles the delay time, like a tape or a chorus, with the left and right repeats moving out of step so the delay also sounds wider in STEREO and LAYER (in DUEL each part's delay just wobbles). DEPTH 0 is off and the default: every existing sound and saved project plays exactly as before. RATE is faded while DEPTH is 0. It needs no extra memory and costs nothing while off.
- **WAVE, a wavetable engine.** A new engine, after SLICE in the engine list. TABLE picks one of 14 tables (saw to square, pulse width, harmonics, odd harmonics, organ drawbars, vowels, brass, bowed string, resonant sweep, comb, FM digital, glass, chimes, rough), POS moves through it, BEND bends the wave's shape, SYNC adds hard sync, SUB a sine an octave down, DTN a second detuned copy. The envelopes, the LFOs and the matrix move POS through their SHP depths; CUT and RES are the universal filter, as on PHASE. A table is made from a short recipe the moment it is chosen, so the part is silent for a moment (usually a few hundredths of a second) and then fades in; high notes stay clean (no harsh aliasing). Eleven new factory sounds: PWM PAD, VOWEL PAD, GLASS PAD, EVOLVING PAD, DRAWBARS, CHIMES, SYNTH BRASS, DIGI PLUCK, SYNC LEAD, HARM BASS and RESO SWEEP (108 factory sounds now; they come last in their categories, so the list places after them move). Every other engine, sound and saved setting plays as before.

### Changed
- **The universal filter now works with CUT at OFF.** On PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, GRAIN, PHYS, FM6 and SLICE, an envelope, an LFO, the modulation matrix or aftertouch routed to the filter now moves it even when the CUT knob is in the centre (OFF); before, the routes did nothing until you turned CUT away from OFF. The filter adds the knob and all the modulation together: below zero it is a low-pass closing, above zero a high-pass, as on the knob. It is bypassed only when the total is exactly zero, so a sound with CUT OFF and no filter route is unchanged, bit for bit. The ENV, LFO and ENV 2 / LFO 2 FLT cards and RES are no longer greyed out on these engines (SET CUT is gone), and the delay modulation's RATE is no longer greyed at DEPTH 0 (SET DEP is gone): a control is greyed only when a selection makes it impossible, never because its value is zero. CPU: when only a route is active the filter costs the same as with the knob on (about 44 instructions per voice and sample on PHASE and WHEEL). Factory sounds keep their sound: on the eight universal-filter engines their FLT routes (which did nothing while CUT was OFF) now start at 0, so every factory sound plays exactly as in 0.2.4; for 16 of them the End B seed was re-picked (the roll draws changed with the removed routes). A MORPH roll still never switches the filter on by itself when CUT is OFF: SMART keeps a FLT depth at 0 and adds no CUT route (WILD may).
- **The LEDS modes are now called OFF, LOW, MEDIUM, MAX** (before: OFF, DIM LO, DIM HI, INV). MEDIUM is still the default, MAX is still the stock look, and saved settings and the editor keep working. MEDIUM is now clearly brighter: the idle buttons and keys are lit a quarter of the time (25 % of full) instead of a faint glow, LOW stays the faintest glow (under 2 %).
- **Scale lights tuned.** The scale's keys are now lit at about half of full, clearly dimmer than the root (full) and well above MEDIUM. Keys outside the scale follow OFF-SCALE KEYS: with SNAP they stay dark, with PLAY (they play their own note) they glow faintly at the LOW level so you can see them. The DRUM grid's beat keys with LEDS MEDIUM are lit at the same 5/6 level, above the MEDIUM keys.
- **The ducker's pump is tuned for house and techno (120 to 140 BPM).** Turn DEPTH up on a part with the built-in PUMP source and nothing else touched: it ducks on every quarter note and the sound comes back the way a compressor lets go, fast at first and then settling smoothly (CURVE now defaults to EXP; LIN, the straight-line release, is still a choice). HOLD is gone from the DUCK pages (a pump is just attack and release; DUCK 2/2 is RLS and CURVE); an old stored HOLD value is kept but no longer does anything. PUMP now keeps counting the beats while DEPTH is 0, so raising DEPTH later ducks on the beat of the transport and not at the moment you turn the knob. Saved patches, multis and projects keep the curve they had; the nine factory sounds that use the ducker now get the smoother EXP release. The normal (SMART) morph roll of a part with the ducker on now moves only DEPTH, so the tuned attack, release and threshold stay; WILD still rolls them. This also changes the End B of those nine sounds (they keep their seeds).
- The dual-mono output mode SPLIT is now called DUEL, because LAYER has a key SPLIT. Only the name changed: saved data and MIDI are unaffected.

### Fixed
- USB LEVEL FIXED no longer overdrives the limiter; USB now carries the mix at the default MASTER level. The speaker and headphones still follow MASTER (above the default position the loudest peaks are clipped at full scale, never wrapped).

## DUELL 0.2.4 beta (first firmware beta)

**The first firmware release: the device package `duell-0.2.4-beta.fwsc`, the installer page and the browser emulator `duell-emulator-0.2.4-beta.zip`. It covers everything since 0.1 beta (the earlier builds 0.2.1 to 0.2.3 were never published). Tried on one FM-1, the author's; install at your own risk; no warranty; not affiliated with M-VAVE.**

### New (since 0.1 beta)
- **Chords and one global scale.** SCALE and ROOT are now device settings (default CHROMATIC, root C), not part of a patch, part, multi or factory sound. SCL button, page 1 SCALE (K1 SCALE, K2 ROOT, K3 OFF-SCALE) and page 2 CHORD (K1 CHRD, K2 VOIC, K3 TRN, the selected part's). OFF-SCALE KEYS: SNAP (default) moves a key or MIDI note outside the scale to the nearest scale note (a tie goes down), PLAY lets it pass. Both parts sound the same note for a key. DIA3 / DIA7 chords stack the scale's degrees; with the scale CHROMATIC they play a plain MAJ triad / MAJ7 and the card says so. The root of a chord is never re-pitched.
- **Universal filter** for the engines that had none of their own (PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, GRAIN, PHYS, FM6, SLICE), on a new EDIT > FILTER page: K1 CUT is one bipolar knob (centre OFF, left a low-pass, right a high-pass), K2 RES. Envelopes, LFOs, the modulation matrix and MIDI CC74 (CUT, 64 = OFF) and CC71 (RES) drive it; while CUT is OFF the sound is unchanged. ANALOG, TRIO, NOISE and SAMPLE keep their own filters.
- **SAVE flow.** A short press of SAVE opens the page that fits: PATTERNS from a sequencer page (STEP, GRID, AUTOMATION), otherwise MULTIS in LAYER and PATCHES in SPLIT and STEREO. **Hold SAVE saves over** the patch you are editing (BOTH patches on the MORPH page), and now also over the pattern or the multi picked on the PATTERNS and MULTIS lists ("SAVE OVER <name>?", OCT+ yes; an empty multi asks for a name first). Page buttons leave and cancel any dialog without writing. The PATCHES list shows at most three empty places at its end; FREE nn still shows the true number.
- **ERASE DATA** (MENU > SYSTEM > ERASE DATA, OCT+ asks, OCT+ yes) erases settings, favourites, autosave, your patches, multis and pattern slots (not your user samples) and restarts the unit as new (VIOLET, LAYER, M01). The installer has an optional tick box "Erase my saved data after installing" (set by what the unit runs, see Changed). A plain reinstall still keeps your data.
- **GLO page.** GLO is now GLO, MIDI, SIDECHAIN, MIDI LEARN, TEMPO. The keys play notes there. K1 MUTE 1 and K2 MUTE 2 act on one click (before: three clicks), K3 LEVEL 1, K4 LEVEL 2, with a meter per part; new TEMPO page (tempo, swing, tap) and MIDI page (channels, MIDI IN, clock source). MORPH's ROLL also acts on one click (A left, B right). Every part starts unmuted at power-on.
- **VIOLET theme** (the default) is now the darker, near-black, more saturated violet and pink.
- **Menu.** Page buttons (EDIT, FX, ENV, LFO, ARP, SEQ, SCL, GLO, SAVE) now work inside the MENU and close it. Menu settings are saved when they change (about 2 s after the knob rests). ABOUT shows the version, build date, USB link and CPU line.
- **Header and footer redone:** the page name sits in the top middle of every screen; the footer shows the engine, the full patch name and the page number.
- **Factory sounds and multis.** Four new factory multis M01 EERIE SIGNAL, M02 WIRED SNAIL, M03 DEAD BUFFER, M04 ACID ETHER. Several factory sounds were revised after audition on the device: #19 HORN SOLO, #22 SILVER FLUTE (no click at the loop wrap), #65 MOTOR VIBES, #66 ROSEWOOD, #67 OPEN SIZZLE, #68 STATIC SNARE, #72 WOOFER, #97 renamed SNARE BUZZ. LFO TRIG now defaults to FREE. Because of the new filter, 46 factory sounds on the filterless engines no longer move their tone controls from the cutoff routes (to be checked by ear).
- **Installer page.** BACK UP, INSTALL DUELL and RETURN TO OFFICIAL FIRMWARE are three separate cards with their own progress, a banner names the running action, the other buttons are locked meanwhile and closing the tab asks first.
- **One version source** (the `VERSION` file): the device (splash, ABOUT), the installer and the emulator show "DUELL 0.2.4 beta".

### Changed or removed
- Installer: the erase box is chosen by what the unit runs: ticked for a clean install unless DUELL is detected.
- The FX hold layer's performance effects are gone (REPEAT, REVERSE, TAPE STOP, FREEZE, OCT UP / DN, master FLANGER and PHASER, CRUSH, THROW, the master FILTER macro, black-key track mutes, MENU > CONTROL > FX LATCH). A short press of FX still opens the FX pages, holding it is the same, and keys pressed with FX held are notes. The GLO mutes, the metronome, the per-part INSERT and the master chain are as before.
- FLAT is the only look: MENU > DISPLAY > STYLE, LARGE and MENU > SYSTEM > HELP are removed (saved settings still load).
- The CPU test screen and the TEST multis M13..M16 are not in the release build (they remain a developer switch, DUELL_TEST_MODE). INFO is gone from the menu.
- SCALE LINK, the per-part SCALE / ROOT, QNT (OFF / SNAP / WHITE / SEQ) and SOLO are replaced by the global scale, OFF-SCALE KEYS and MUTE.

### Fixed
- Load-time CPU spikes: loading a patch or a multi, or changing the mode or the reverb, no longer misses audio blocks in normal use (very heavy multis can still click, see KNOWN_ISSUES). PHASE is much cheaper (PHASE + ANALOG measured on the unit: 63 % average, 71 % peak, 0 missed).
- LFO routes to PAN, DIST, sends and engine macros did nothing audible with an arp; an arp is now one LFO phrase.
- The arp now follows an external clock (USB / TRS) that runs without a Start; a Stop makes it wait again. The TEMPO and MIDI pages show NO CLK when the external clock sends nothing for 1 s.
- SPLIT checked for leakage over every send, reverb, insert, EQ, ducker and DIST (the other channel is digital silence); stereo-only controls that SPLIT does not use are shown faded with the reason SPLIT.
- A restored autosave never brings back a muted part.

### Compatibility
- As 0.1 beta: back up first; patches, MULTIs and PATTERNs saved with a beta may not load in other betas. Old patches read CUT OFF, RES 0.
- The device backup does not include MULTIs or PATTERN slots; restore is untested (see KNOWN_ISSUES).

## DUELL 0.1 beta (emulator release; firmware to follow)

**First public beta: Felucca turned into a two-part, morphing synth for the FM-1. The browser emulator is published first. The firmware has been run on one real FM-1 by the author; the firmware package follows after more hardware testing (including the return to the official firmware, which is untested).**

### New (compared to Felucca)
- Two parts, with SPLIT, STEREO and LAYER modes (BALANCE, velocity XFADE, key SPLIT).
- MORPH between End A and End B, with smart random (ROLL A or B, UNDO, one AMOUNT), saved with the patch.
- PATCHES (one list, factory and yours; up to 48 stored patches, FREE nn on the card), 16 MULTIs, 16 named PATTERNs.
- ENV 2 and LFO 2, 8-slot modulation matrix.
- Effects: per-part EQ, per-part ducker with MIDI/pulse/part sidechain, ping-pong delay, SHIM and RESO reverbs (from JIANT).
- MIDI: a channel per part, clock in, program change and bank select, a CC map, mod wheel to morph.
- All MIDI settings on GLO's pages (MIDI with P1 CH, P2 CH, MIDI IN OWN CH / SELECTED and the clock source CLK; SIDECHAIN; MIDI LEARN with CLEAR), none in MENU. GLO's pages in order: GLO, MIDI, SIDECHAIN, MIDI LEARN, TEMPO (K1 TEMPO, K2 SWG, K3 TAP: a detent either way is one tap), TEST.
- GLO's main page no longer takes over the keys: they play notes. K1 MUTE and K2 SOLO turn left for part 1 and right for part 2 (one action per twist, the MORPH page's gesture), K3 LEVEL 1, K4 LEVEL 2, with a dB meter per part between the columns. MENU re-audited: OVERVIEW (SCOPE / PARTS) and HOME OPENS (MORPH, MODE, MIXER, OVERVIEW), INFO and ABOUT also open with KNOB 1. A new unit starts in VIOLET, LAYER, with factory multi M01 loaded.
- A sound palette sorted by category, nine colour themes, a navigation scheme where SELECT selects and knobs adjust.
- A CPU load meter (MENU > SYSTEM > INFO: 1 s average, peak hold, missed blocks), a power-on splash (DUELL title block over the morphing wave lines, any key skips), an ABOUT page with the title block, version and Felucca credit. VIOLET is the default theme.

ABOUT now opens on the splash animation (SELECT scrolls to the information).
