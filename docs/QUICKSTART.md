# DUELL quick start

**DUELL**
MultiMorphing Bitimbral Synthesizer
by OSCILL/R

DUELL turns the FM-1 into two synths in one box. This page is the short tour; [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) is the full design. DUELL 1.0 runs on the FM-1 ([INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)) ([web emulator](https://oscillr.github.io/DUELL/emu/)).


## The idea
- **Two parts**, 1 and 2. Each has its own sound, effects, EQ, ducker and arp. The **ALGORITHM** knob picks which part you are editing.
- **Three modes** (HOME, second sub-page, first knob: MODE):
  - **DUEL** (default): part 1 hard left, part 2 hard right, kept apart, effects included. For playing two sounds into two channels.
  - **STEREO**: both parts in one stereo field, for headphones.
  - **LAYER**: both parts play on the keys together. The MODE page has BALANCE, XFADE (velocity crossfade) and SPLIT (a key split point).
- **MORPH**: every sound has two ends, A and B, and one fader between them.

## The first-start guide
The first time DUELL starts (and once after an update from an older build) a short guide follows the logo: six pages on the modes, MORPH, the long presses, REC as HOLD, the OCT- reset, STOP ALL and SAVE OVER. Turn **SELECT** (or press **OCT+** / **OCT-**) to go through it; **HOME** closes it, and any page button closes it and opens its page. The keys play while it is shown. To see it again: MENU (hold HOME) > SYSTEM > GUIDE.

## The controls in one minute
- **SELECT** switches sub-pages (and moves rows in lists). It never changes the tempo. **Knobs 1 to 4** adjust the four columns on screen. Every control does what its label says.
- **PRESETS** loads sounds for the selected part. A sound brings its own loudness (its **PATCH LEVEL**, saved with the patch, on EDIT > VOICE 2/2 knob 4); your volume on the mixer (the **LEVEL** cards of the GLO MIXER page, and MIDI CC7) is yours: loading a sound never changes it, and a multi remembers it. The part plays at the patch level and the level together.
- **HOME** opens the MORPH page. Hold HOME for the menu (ABOUT shows the version and credits).
- **MIXER** (GLO's first page since the 0.2.25 layout round; HOME's round is MORPH, MORPH SET, MODE): both parts side by side, part 1 left and part 2 right, with the level meters between them. Knob 1 is LEVEL 1, knob 2 PAN 1, knob 3 LEVEL 2, knob 4 PAN 2 (in DUEL the pans are fixed and not shown). Each part's cell reads MUTE and is lit in the part's colour while the part is muted; hold GLO and press **OCT-** or **OCT+** to mute or unmute part 1 or part 2. SPACE is on the PERFORM page.
- **TEMPO** is on the GLO TEMPO page, the one after MIXER (a tap of GLO opens MIXER first). To set the levels and pans of both parts from any page hold GLO: the keys play notes, knob 1 is LEVEL 1, knob 2 PAN 1, knob 3 LEVEL 2, knob 4 PAN 2 (in DUEL the pans are fixed and not shown). With GLO held, **OCT-** mutes or unmutes part 1 and **OCT+** part 2; each cell reads MUTE, lit in the part's colour while muted (the mutes moved to these keys in 0.2.24; before, knobs 1 and 2 were the mutes).
- **OCT-/OCT+** change octave when you let go of them; in dialogs OCT- means back and OCT+ means do it. Hold **OCT-** and turn a knob to reset that knob's card to its default (the octave then does not change).

## The menu
Hold **HOME**. Four tabs: **DISPLAY** (COLOR, LEDS, SCALE LEDS, ANIMATION, SCREEN OFF, CPU METER), **CONTROL** (MULTI LOAD, HOME OPENS, KNOB ACCEL, BUTTON HOLD, STEP KEYS, STEP PREVIEW, PERF LEAVE), **AUDIO** (CLICK, CLICK LEVEL, COUNT-IN, TUNE: the frequency of A4 in Hz like a Roland keyboard, 415.3 to 466.2 Hz in steps of 0.1 Hz, 440.0 at first; with KNOB ACCEL on, a fast turn moves several steps) and **SYSTEM** (RESTORE LAST, BUTTON MAP, GUIDE, ABOUT, ERASE DATA; developer builds have a USB SERIAL row too). Turn **SELECT** to go from row to row (on through the tabs), turn any knob or press OCT+ / OCT- to change the value; HOME closes it. A tab with more rows than fit shows a second page (SYSTEM 1/2 in its tab). **BASS MONO** is a sound setting, so it is not in the MENU: it is on the **GLO** button's round (the page **STEREO MASTER**, after SHIMMER; KNOB 1; 200 Hz on a new unit). It makes the output mono below the frequency you pick (40 to 400 Hz; 100 to 150 Hz is the usual club setting) so wide effects do not smear the bass; it belongs to the unit (a multi does not change it), it works in STEREO and LAYER, and in DUEL its card is not shown.
- Defaults worth knowing: KNOB ACCEL ON, CLICK REC (the metronome only while you record on the sequencer pages), USB SERIAL OFF (with it ON, macOS 13 to 15 do not show the USB audio input). MULTI LOAD KEEPS MODE: loading a multi keeps your MODE, BALANCE, XFADE and SPLIT; set SETS MODE to take the multi's.

## Make a first sound
1. Press **PRESETS** and turn it to hear sounds. Press **ALGORITHM** to select part 2 and load another.
2. Go to the MODE page and try DUEL, STEREO, LAYER.
3. Press **HOME** for the MORPH page. Knob 1 moves the morph from A to B. Knob 2 rolls a new random end with one click (turn left = A, right = B). Knob 3 undoes the last roll of A or B. Knob 4 is AMOUNT, the one percentage: how far a roll may go. On this page **ALGORITHM** has three positions, PART 1, BOTH and PART 2: with BOTH the morph moves both parts and both part numbers in the header light up.
4. Press **SAVE**. The first page is **PATCHES**: 128 slots, #1 to #128, and the factory sounds fill them. **Hold SAVE** asks "OVERWRITE #12 <name>?" (the slot your sound came from); turn **knob 1** in the dialog to pick another slot, turn **knob 2 (RENAME)** to give the sound a new name first (the NAME screen opens; OCT+ there brings you back to the dialog with the new name), OCT+ says yes. Whatever was in that slot is replaced. Knob 4 **RESTORE** takes your sound out of the slot again and the factory sound comes back.
   **Tip:** a short press of SAVE opens the page that fits where you are: PATTERNS when you are on a sequencer page (STEP, GRID, AUTOMATION), otherwise MULTIS in LAYER and PATCHES in DUEL and STEREO. SELECT moves between PATCHES, PATTERNS, MULTIS and TOOLS. Hold SAVE on PATTERNS or MULTIS saves over the slot you picked ("SAVE OVER <name>?", OCT+ yes); an empty multi slot asks for a name first.

## Saving things
- **PATCHES**: one list of 128 slots. A slot holds the sound you saved there, or else its factory sound. Knob 1 steps the slots, knob 2 filters by type, knob 3 marks favourites, knob 4 RESTORE brings the factory sound back (the card is not shown when the slot holds the factory sound already). **SAVE > TOOLS** has RESTORE ALL (every slot gets its factory sound). MIDI program change n loads slot n. Saving needs the transport stopped.
- **PATTERNS**: 16 named, overwritable step patterns.
- **MULTIS**: 16 slots that hold both parts, effects, arps, patterns, mode and morph settings. M01 to M16 are factory multis; M16 STRESS TEST is a CPU test, not music, and free to overwrite. Loading a multi sets its own levels, pan, mute and sends and the effects it stores (chorus, delay, reverb with their settings), whatever the mixer was; it leaves your MIDI settings, the clock source, TUNE and the master effect BASS MONO as they are (BASS MONO is a setting of your unit, not of the multi).
- To start from scratch (erases settings, patches, multis, patterns; keeps the FM6 voice bank): MENU (hold HOME) > SYSTEM > ERASE DATA. A reinstall alone keeps your data.
- **WAVE user tables (new in 0.2.14):** WAVE's TABLE knob has three places after the factory tables, USR1 to USR3, named EMPTY until you fill them (an empty one plays a sine). You make a table in the editor from a WAV file (a single-cycle wave, or a wavetable file like the ones Serum and Vital export) and send it to a slot; the unit keeps it, ERASE DATA erases it, an update does not. **A user table is reduced to 19 frames and 64 harmonics and the phases are not kept**, so it sounds like the file's spectrum, not exactly like the file. A patch that uses USR2 on a unit where USR2 is empty plays a sine. The editor's WAVETABLES tab does this; see [EDITOR_LIBRARIAN.md](EDITOR_LIBRARIAN.md).
- **Patch library in the editor (new in 0.2.20):** the editor's PATCHES tab exports one patch, a selection or the whole bank to a file, imports patch files and **DX7 `.syx` files** (each voice becomes an FM6 patch) with a preview and your choice of slots, copies and swaps slots by dragging, and loads a dropped patch into a part without saving. The WAVETABLES tab takes dropped WAV files and shares tables as small files. See [EDITOR_LIBRARIAN.md](EDITOR_LIBRARIAN.md).
- The device backup (installer page, and the editor's Backup page) holds patches, MULTIs, PATTERNs, the user wavetables, settings and the current music. **Back up before you update:** patches, multis and projects saved with 0.2.13 or earlier do not load in 0.2.20. This beta's saves may also not load in other betas.

## Playing and modulation
- **ARP**, **SEQ**, **PLAY/STOP**, **REC**: each part has an arp; the SEQUENCER arp mode plays a 16-step pattern. REC arms recording on the sequencer pages (STEP, AUTOMATION) and in the SEQ layer; on every other page a tap of REC is the HOLD switch for the part or parts the keys play (both in LAYER; its light is bright when HOLD is on). While a part is armed for recording, a red dot and REC show in the bottom row of every page, next to the page number. PLAY/STOP lights green when stopped and red when playing (it blinks red on each beat of the count-in). When stopped, a short press of PLAY/STOP starts on its release; holding it for 0.6 s (or pressing it twice) is STOP ALL: it silences everything, the delay, chorus and reverb tails too (a single press of STOP leaves the tails to ring out).
- Two envelopes, two LFOs and an 8-slot modulation matrix per part. ENV 1 is the amplitude envelope, FILTER ENV the filter envelope.
- **Filters:** the engines' own filter (ANALOG's VCF, TRIO FILTER, NOISE FILT) has its page in EDIT, and every engine has the multimode filter after it: **MULTI FILTER 1/2** is CUT, RES, MODE and SLOPE; **MULTI FILTER 2/2** is KTRK (the cutoff follows the note you play, -100 to +100 %; a new or initialised sound and most factory sounds start at +51 %, the basses, percussion and kicks at 0), and where FILTER ENV, LFO 1 and LFO 2 drive this filter their three amounts (FILTER ENV, LFO 1, LFO 2); the same values are on the DEST pages of those modules (FILTER ENV DEST, LFO 1 DEST, LFO 2 DEST).
- **Quick edit:** hold **EDIT**, **LFO**, **ENV**, **ARP** or **GLO** for a moment and the four knobs become performance controls (header QUICK MULTI, QUICK LFO, QUICK ENV, QUICK ARP, QUICK GLO with a small bolt; the header shows the one part they edit). EDIT: cutoff, resonance, filter mode (the multimode filter, the same on every engine), release. LFO: each LFO's rate and its amount on the cutoff. ENV: attack, release, the filter envelope's amount and decay. ARP: mode, rate, gate, octaves. GLO: level and pan of part 1 and part 2 (the mutes on OCT- / OCT+), with the level meters, also over PERFORM. In every quick layer the keys still play. Let go and you are back on your page; a short press opens the page as always.
- **FX** (the part's own pages: FX, INSERT, EQ, DUCK; the last page, SEND FX, shows what the delay (mode and time) and the reverb (type and size) are set to, and a CHORUS card; it shows no send levels (those are on the FX page); under the cards it says which effect is INACTIVE for this part (its send is 0) and TURN = JUMP TO SETTINGS: turn knob 1, 2 or 3 to jump to the CHORUS, DELAY 1/2 or REVERB page of GLO (0.2.24). Chorus, delay and reverb, being the instrument's, are pages of the GLO button since 0.2.21): insert, per-part EQ, per-part **ducker** (SRC: OFF, a built-in pulse (PUMP), the other part, or MIDI, a note on the channel set on GLO > SIDECHAIN; a new sound starts at OFF and shows only SRC until you pick a source (0.2.24); with SRC MIDI, knob 4 of DUCK 1/2 jumps to that page, which says each part's SRC), per-part EQ (EQ 1/2: LOW FREQ, LOW GAIN, MID FREQ, MID GAIN; EQ 2/2: MID Q, HIGH FREQ, HIGH GAIN; a band at 0 dB runs no EQ code; the panel draws the curve of the three bands; the ON / OFF switch of 0.2.24 is gone in the 0.2.25 layout round), chorus (new in 0.2.21: in STEREO and LAYER the chorus is stereo, the left and the right of the sound sweep a quarter turn apart; RATE and DEPTH work as before, and in DUEL the chorus stays as it was, on each part's own side), delay (DELAY 1/2 starts with MODE: DIGITAL, the plain delay; PING-PONG, the repeats alternate left and right; and, new in 0.2.14, RANDOM, where on a random share of the beats the repeat is retriggered, reversed, or an octave up or down, and OCT UP, octave-up repeats. In RANDOM and OCT UP the MOD card on DELAY 2/2 (the delay's modulation depth) is CHANCE, the share of beats (40 % when you switch in with it at 0), the beat is the delay's TIME, the MRATE card (the modulation rate) is not shown (the column stays empty), and the repeats do not alternate left and right, that is PING-PONG. SPREAD is the stereo width, not shown in DIGITAL (nor in DUEL, where there is nothing to spread). The pages run top-down: MODE, TIME, FDBK, COLR, then SPREAD, MRATE, MOD, MIX last. In DUEL (new in 0.2.22) all four modes work, mono on each part's own side: PING-PONG sounds like DIGITAL there, because a ping-pong needs two sides, and the RANDOM and OCT UP events stay in the middle of the part's side), reverbs (REVERB page, TYPE: ROOM, SPRING, HALL, SHIM, RESO and, new in 0.2.14, PLATE: a smooth stereo plate; SIZE is how long the tail lasts, DAMP how dark it gets, and, new in 0.2.14, LOCUT on K4 cuts the bass that goes into the reverb so it does not get muddy: OFF or 20 Hz to 2 kHz, 299 Hz by default (0.2.22: it was up to 450 Hz and 88 Hz); it works on every reverb type). SHIM and RESO reverbs come from JIANT. New in 0.2.19: with TYPE SHIM, a second page SHIMMER follows REVERB (it is not shown for the other types, the GLO button and SELECT skip it): PITCH sets the shimmered part, UP (an octave up, as before) or DOWN (an octave down; BOTH was removed in 0.2.25), and DRIFT (0 to 100 %, off by default; the card was MOD before the 0.2.25 layout round) lets its pitch drift slowly so the shimmer does not stand still; 40 is a gentle drift, 100 a wide one. Both are saved with the multi.

**PERFORM.** Hold **FX** for a moment (or hold FX and press a white key) to open the PERFORM page. Now the 16 white keys are effects that last while you hold them, four per group of keys: REPEAT 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 and REVERSE; TAPE STOP, FREEZE, OCT UP and OCT DN; FLANGER, PHASER, DRIVE and PUMP; ROLL A and ROLL B (a new MORPH end, as on the MORPH page), MUTE 1 and MUTE 2. The black keys still play notes, and in PERFORM they play in the scale (they follow the SCALE and ROOT of the SCALE page even if OFF-SCALE says PLAY; with SCALE CHROMATIC they play as they are). Knob 1 is MORPH, knob 2 SPACE (left a smaller room, right a huge one), knob 3 KILL (left low-pass, right high-pass, OFF in the middle), knob 4 DEPTH (how strong the held effects are). Here ALGORITHM picks PART 1, BOTH or PART 2, the same setting as on the MORPH page (PRESETS changes the sound of that part, or of both, each from its own sound), and SELECT is BALANCE between the two parts. Knob 2 SPACE is the same SPACE as the third knob of the MIXER page. Tap FX to go back to where you were: SPACE and KILL then fade back to neutral in under half a second, and they do the same when you load another patch or multi. If you want them to stay as you left them, set MENU > CONTROL > PERF LEAVE to KEEP.

**MODULES.** On the GLO pages (tap GLO, then SELECT: MIXER, TEMPO, CHORUS, DELAY 1/2, DELAY 2/2, REVERB, SHIMMER with TYPE SHIM, STEREO MASTER, COMPRESSOR, MODULES, SIDECHAIN, MIDI, MIDI LEARN; these pages draw in a third colour of their own, neither part's, and ALGORITHM does nothing on them) four switches turn whole parts of DUELL off for the unit: knob 1 ARP, knob 2 SEQ, knob 3 SENDS, knob 4 PART FX, each ON (the default) or OFF (turn left for OFF, right for ON). ARP OFF: the keys play directly, no arp. SEQ OFF: PLAY runs the tempo and the arps but no stored steps (you can still edit them). SENDS OFF: no chorus, delay or reverb, which saves a lot of CPU. PART FX OFF: no SAT, insert, EQ or ducker. They are kept when you power off but are not part of a patch or a multi; your settings on the other pages stay as they are, and the cards a module switches off are not shown (their column stays empty and their knob does nothing).

**SIDECHAIN.** The GLO page SIDECHAIN is the MIDI setup of the ducker, for both parts: knob 1 PART 1 CH and knob 2 PART 1 NOTE, knob 3 PART 2 CH and knob 4 PART 2 NOTE (the channel, default 10, and the note, default C2 or ANY). A part whose DUCK source (FX > DUCK 1/2, SRC) is MIDI ducks when that note arrives on that channel; the page lists each part's source (PART 1 PUMP, PART 2 MIDI ...). These four belong to your unit like the MIDI channels: a patch or a multi never changes them, and you can set them with any source.

**COMPRESSOR (new in 0.2.21 as COMP).** A compressor on the whole output, on its own GLO page after STEREO MASTER (tap GLO, then SELECT). Four knobs: **RATIO** (OFF, 1.5:1, 2:1, 3:1, 4:1, 6:1, 8:1, 12:1, INF; knob 1 since the 0.2.25 layout round), **THRESH** (-40 to 0 dB), **ATTACK** (0.1 to 100 ms) and **RELEASE** (20 ms to 2 s), the two times in fine steps since 0.2.24 (40 a decade; the defaults 10 ms and 200 ms as before). It starts at **RATIO OFF** (the old 1:1): the sound is exactly what it was, and only RATIO is shown; turn RATIO up and the compressor is on and the other three appear. The panel draws the curve (input across, output up) and a bar of how much it turns the sound down right now. The makeup gain is automatic: a signal at -8 dB stays as loud as it was, so switching it on does not make the music louder. It squeezes the left and right together (one gain for both), with a soft knee. A slow ATTACK lets the start of a note through; a quick RELEASE can pump. Start with 2:1 or 3:1, THRESH a little under your loudest peaks, ATTACK 10 ms, RELEASE 200 ms. It works in STEREO and LAYER, not in DUEL (the cards are not shown there). It belongs to the unit, not to a multi: it is kept when you power off but a multi load does not change it, and ERASE DATA resets it to off.

**The STEREO MASTER page (0.2.23, 0.2.25).** One GLO page, **STEREO MASTER**, holds **BASS MONO** (knob 1; it starts ON at 200 Hz on a new unit and belongs to the unit, not to a multi or a patch) and **WIDTH** (knob 2); the panel shows the mono region below BASS MONO and the width above it. (Up to the 0.2.25 layout round WIDTH and MASTER were two pages.) COMPRESSOR follows it. **WIDTH** (0 to 200 %) makes the stereo picture narrower or wider: 100 % is off, 0 % is mono, 200 % is twice as wide. Wider only works above 125 Hz, so the bass is never spread, and the mono sum never changes. WIDTH starts off (the sound is exactly what it was), works in STEREO and LAYER (in DUEL the page says it is not used there), and belongs to the unit like the COMPRESSOR: kept when you power off, not changed by a multi load, reset by ERASE DATA.

**Piano roll (editor, new in 0.2.20).** In the web editor each part has a PIANO ROLL tab for its step pattern, and a ROLL button that opens it across the whole window. See [EDITOR_ROLL.md](EDITOR_ROLL.md).

## MIDI
Part 1 listens on channel 1 and part 2 on channel 2 (editable). On the device: clock in, program change and bank select, the mod wheel (CC1) moves the MORPH fader and is also a matrix source. CC 20 morph, 21 roll A, 22 roll B, 23 undo, 25 amount, 111 morph target, 112 mode. In LAYER, program change 0 to 15 on part 1's channel recalls MULTIs M01 to M16. Not yet tested with a range of real MIDI devices.
