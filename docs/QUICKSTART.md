# DUELL quick start

**DUELL**
MultiMorphing Bitimbral Synthesizer
by OSCILL/R

DUELL beta turns the FM-1 into two synths in one box. This page is the short tour; [SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) is the full design. DUELL 0.2.20 beta runs on the FM-1 ([INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)) and in the [hosted browser emulator](https://oscillr.github.io/DUELL/emu/) (click the page once to start the sound); the controls below are the same in both.

In the emulator your saved patches, MULTIs and PATTERNs stay in the browser. Press "Download saved data" in the emulator page to back them up, and clear the site data after an emulator update if saved data misbehaves.

## The idea
- **Two parts**, 1 and 2. Each has its own sound, effects, EQ, ducker and arp. The **ALGORITHM** knob picks which part you are editing.
- **Three modes** (HOME, second sub-page, first knob: MODE):
  - **DUEL** (default): part 1 hard left, part 2 hard right, kept apart, effects included. For playing two sounds into two channels.
  - **STEREO**: both parts in one stereo field, for headphones.
  - **LAYER**: both parts play on the keys together. The MODE page has BALANCE, XFADE (velocity crossfade) and SPLIT (a key split point).
- **MORPH**: every sound has two ends, A and B, and one fader between them.

## The first-start guide
The first time DUELL starts (and once after an update from an older build) a short guide follows the logo: six pages on the modes, MORPH, the long presses, REC as HOLD, the OCT- reset, STOP ALL and SAVE OVER. Turn **SELECT** (or press **OCT+** / **OCT-**) to go through it; **HOME** closes it, and any page button closes it and opens its page. The keys play while it is shown. To see it again: MENU (hold HOME) > SYSTEM > GUIDE.

## The controls in one minute
- **SELECT** switches sub-pages (and moves rows in lists). It never changes the tempo. **Knobs 1 to 4** adjust the four columns on screen. Every control does what its label says.
- **PRESETS** loads sounds for the selected part. A sound brings its own loudness (its **TRIM**, saved with the patch, on EDIT > VOICE 2/2 knob 4); your volume on the mixer (the **LEVEL** card of the MIXER page and the GLO page, and MIDI CC7) is yours: loading a sound never changes it, and a multi remembers it. The part plays at the trim and the level together.
- **HOME** opens the MORPH page. Hold HOME for the menu (ABOUT shows the version and credits).
- **MIXER** (HOME, fourth page: MORPH, MORPH SET, MODE, MIXER): both parts side by side, part 1 left and part 2 right, each with its sound, level, pan, reverb, delay and chorus sends and mute, and the level meters between them. The knobs (MUTE, PAN, SPACE, LEVEL) edit the selected part, whose side has the bright outline; **ALGORITHM** switches the part.
- **TEMPO** is on the GLO TEMPO page, knob 1. On the GLO page the keys play notes, knob 1 mutes part 1 and knob 2 mutes part 2 with one click (turn again to unmute), knobs 3 and 4 are the levels.
- **OCT-/OCT+** change octave when you let go of them; in dialogs OCT- means back and OCT+ means do it. Hold **OCT-** and turn a knob to reset that knob's card to its default (the octave then does not change).

## The menu
Hold **HOME**. Four tabs: **DISPLAY** (COLOR, LEDS, SCALE LEDS, ANIMATION, SCREEN OFF, CPU METER), **CONTROL** (MULTI LOAD, HOME OPENS, KNOB ACCEL, BUTTON HOLD, STEP KEYS, STEP PREVIEW, PERF LEAVE), **AUDIO** (USB LEVEL, CLICK, CLICK LEVEL, COUNT-IN, TUNE) and **SYSTEM** (RESTORE LAST, BUTTON MAP, USB SERIAL, GUIDE, ABOUT, ERASE DATA). Turn **SELECT** to go from row to row (on through the tabs), turn any knob or press OCT+ / OCT- to change the value; HOME closes it. A tab with more rows than fit shows a second page (SYSTEM 1/2 in its tab). **BASS MONO** is a sound setting, so it is not in the MENU: it is on the **GLO** button's round (the page **BASS MONO**, after SIDECHAIN; KNOB 1; OFF at first). It makes the output mono below the frequency you pick (40 to 400 Hz; 100 to 150 Hz is the usual club setting) so wide effects do not smear the bass; it is saved with the multis, it works in STEREO and LAYER, and in DUEL its card is not shown.
- Defaults worth knowing: KNOB ACCEL ON, CLICK REC (the metronome only while you record on the sequencer pages), USB SERIAL OFF (with it ON, macOS 13 to 15 do not show the USB audio input). MULTI LOAD KEEPS MODE: loading a multi keeps your MODE, BALANCE, XFADE and SPLIT; set SETS MODE to take the multi's.

## Make a first sound
1. Press **PRESETS** and turn it to hear sounds. Press **ALGORITHM** to select part 2 and load another.
2. Go to the MODE page and try DUEL, STEREO, LAYER.
3. Press **HOME** for the MORPH page. Knob 1 moves the morph from A to B. Knob 2 rolls a new random end with one click (turn left = A, right = B). Knob 3 undoes the last roll of A or B. Knob 4 is AMOUNT, the one percentage: how far a roll may go. On this page **ALGORITHM** has three positions, PART 1, BOTH and PART 2: with BOTH the morph moves both parts and both part numbers in the header light up.
4. Press **SAVE**. The first page is **PATCHES**: 128 slots, #1 to #128, and the factory sounds fill them. **Hold SAVE** asks "OVERWRITE #12 <name>?" (the slot your sound came from); turn **knob 1** in the dialog to pick another slot, OCT+ says yes. Whatever was in that slot is replaced. Knob 4 **RESTORE** takes your sound out of the slot again and the factory sound comes back.
   **Tip:** a short press of SAVE opens the page that fits where you are: PATTERNS when you are on a sequencer page (STEP, GRID, AUTOMATION), otherwise MULTIS in LAYER and PATCHES in DUEL and STEREO. SELECT moves between PATCHES, PATTERNS, MULTIS and TOOLS. Hold SAVE on PATTERNS or MULTIS saves over the slot you picked ("SAVE OVER <name>?", OCT+ yes); an empty multi slot asks for a name first.

## Saving things
- **PATCHES**: one list of 128 slots. A slot holds the sound you saved there, or else its factory sound. Knob 1 steps the slots, knob 2 filters by type, knob 3 marks favourites, knob 4 RESTORE brings the factory sound back (the card is not shown when the slot holds the factory sound already). **SAVE > TOOLS** has RESTORE ALL (every slot gets its factory sound). MIDI program change n loads slot n. Saving needs the transport stopped.
- **PATTERNS**: 16 named, overwritable step patterns.
- **MULTIS**: 16 slots that hold both parts, effects, arps, patterns, mode and morph settings. M01 to M10 are factory multis.
- To start from scratch (erases settings, patches, multis, patterns; keeps the FM6 voice bank): MENU (hold HOME) > SYSTEM > ERASE DATA. A reinstall alone keeps your data.
- **WAVE user tables (new in 0.2.14):** WAVE's TABLE knob has three places after the factory tables, USR1 to USR3, named EMPTY until you fill them (an empty one plays a sine). You make a table in the editor from a WAV file (a single-cycle wave, or a wavetable file like the ones Serum and Vital export) and send it to a slot; the unit keeps it, ERASE DATA erases it, an update does not. **A user table is reduced to 19 frames and 64 harmonics and the phases are not kept**, so it sounds like the file's spectrum, not exactly like the file. A patch that uses USR2 on a unit where USR2 is empty plays a sine. The editor's WAVETABLES tab does this; see [EDITOR_LIBRARIAN.md](EDITOR_LIBRARIAN.md).
- **Patch library in the editor (new in 0.2.20):** the editor's PATCHES tab exports one patch, a selection or the whole bank to a file, imports patch files and **DX7 `.syx` files** (each voice becomes an FM6 patch) with a preview and your choice of slots, copies and swaps slots by dragging, and loads a dropped patch into a part without saving. The WAVETABLES tab takes dropped WAV files and shares tables as small files. See [EDITOR_LIBRARIAN.md](EDITOR_LIBRARIAN.md).
- The device backup (installer page, and the editor's Backup page) holds patches, MULTIs, PATTERNs, the user wavetables, settings and the current music. **Back up before you update:** patches, multis and projects saved with 0.2.13 or earlier do not load in 0.2.20. This beta's saves may also not load in other betas.

## Playing and modulation
- **ARP**, **SEQ**, **PLAY/STOP**, **REC**: each part has an arp; the SEQUENCER arp mode plays a 16-step pattern. REC arms recording on the sequencer pages (STEP, AUTOMATION) and in the SEQ layer; on every other page a tap of REC is the HOLD switch for the part or parts the keys play (both in LAYER; its light is bright when HOLD is on). While a part is armed for recording, a red dot and REC show in the bottom row of every page, next to the page number. PLAY/STOP lights green when stopped and red when playing (it blinks red on each beat of the count-in). When stopped, a short press of PLAY/STOP starts on its release; holding it for 0.6 s (or pressing it twice) is STOP ALL: it silences everything, the delay, chorus and reverb tails too (a single press of STOP leaves the tails to ring out).
- Two envelopes, two LFOs and an 8-slot modulation matrix per part. ENV 1 is the amplitude envelope, ENV 2 the filter envelope.
- **Quick edit:** hold **EDIT**, **LFO**, **ENV**, **ARP** or **GLO** for a moment and the four knobs become performance controls (header QUICK MULTI, QUICK LFO, QUICK ENV, QUICK ARP, QUICK GLO with a small bolt; the header shows the one part they edit). EDIT: cutoff, resonance, filter mode (the multimode filter, the same on every engine), release. LFO: each LFO's rate and its amount on the cutoff. ENV: attack, release, the filter envelope's amount and decay. ARP: mode, rate, gate, octaves. GLO: mute and level of part 1 and part 2, with the level meters, also over PERFORM. In every quick layer the keys still play. Let go and you are back on your page; a short press opens the page as always.
- **FX**: insert, per-part EQ, per-part **ducker** (sidechain from a MIDI note, a built-in pulse or the other part), chorus, delay (DELAY 1/2 starts with MODE: DIGITAL, the plain delay; PING-PONG, the repeats alternate left and right; and, new in 0.2.14, RANDOM, where on a random share of the beats the repeat is retriggered, reversed, or an octave up or down, and OCT UP, octave-up repeats. In RANDOM and OCT UP the MDEPTH card on DELAY 2/2 (the delay's modulation depth) is CHANCE, the share of beats (40 % when you switch in with it at 0), the beat is the delay's TIME, the MRATE card (the modulation rate) is not shown (the column stays empty), and the repeats do not alternate left and right, that is PING-PONG. SPREAD is the stereo width, not shown in DIGITAL (nor in DUEL, which also hides MODE). The pages run top-down: MODE, TIME, FDBK, COLR, then SPREAD, MRATE, MDEPTH, MIX last. PING-PONG, RANDOM and OCT UP are STEREO and LAYER only), reverbs (REVERB page, TYPE: ROOM, SPRING, HALL, SHIM, RESO and, new in 0.2.14, PLATE: a smooth stereo plate; SIZE is how long the tail lasts, DAMP how dark it gets, and, new in 0.2.14, LOCUT on K4 cuts the bass that goes into the reverb so it does not get muddy: OFF or 20 to 450 Hz, 88 Hz by default; it works on every reverb type). SHIM and RESO reverbs come from JIANT. New in 0.2.19: with TYPE SHIM, a second page SHIMMER follows REVERB (it is not shown for the other types, the FX button and SELECT skip it): PITCH sets the shimmered part, UP (an octave up, as before), DOWN (an octave down) or BOTH, and MOD (0 to 100 %, off by default) lets its pitch drift slowly so the shimmer does not stand still; 40 is a gentle drift, 100 a wide one. Both are saved with the multi.

**PERFORM.** Hold **FX** for a moment (or hold FX and press a white key) to open the PERFORM page. Now the 16 white keys are effects that last while you hold them, four per group of keys: REPEAT 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 and REVERSE; TAPE STOP, FREEZE, OCT UP and OCT DN; FLANGER, PHASER, DRIVE and PUMP; ROLL A and ROLL B (a new MORPH end, as on the MORPH page), MUTE 1 and MUTE 2. The black keys still play notes, and in PERFORM they play in the scale (they follow the SCALE and ROOT of the SCALE page even if OFF-SCALE says PLAY; with SCALE CHROMATIC they play as they are). Knob 1 is MORPH, knob 2 SPACE (left a smaller room, right a huge one), knob 3 KILL (left low-pass, right high-pass, OFF in the middle), knob 4 DEPTH (how strong the held effects are). Here ALGORITHM picks PART 1, BOTH or PART 2, the same setting as on the MORPH page (PRESETS changes the sound of that part, or of both, each from its own sound), and SELECT is BALANCE between the two parts. Knob 2 SPACE is the same SPACE as the third knob of the MIXER page. Tap FX to go back to where you were: SPACE and KILL then fade back to neutral in under half a second, and they do the same when you load another patch or multi. If you want them to stay as you left them, set MENU > CONTROL > PERF LEAVE to KEEP.

**MODULES.** On the GLO pages (tap GLO, then SELECT: GLO, TEMPO, SIDECHAIN, BASS MONO, MIDI, MODULES, MIDI LEARN) four switches turn whole parts of DUELL off for the unit: knob 1 ARP, knob 2 SEQ, knob 3 SENDS, knob 4 PART FX, each ON (the default) or OFF (turn left for OFF, right for ON). ARP OFF: the keys play directly, no arp. SEQ OFF: PLAY runs the tempo and the arps but no stored steps (you can still edit them). SENDS OFF: no chorus, delay or reverb, which saves a lot of CPU. PART FX OFF: no SAT, insert, EQ or ducker. They are kept when you power off but are not part of a patch or a multi; your settings on the other pages stay as they are, and the cards a module switches off are not shown (their column stays empty and their knob does nothing).

**Piano roll (editor, new in 0.2.20).** In the web editor each part has a PIANO ROLL tab for its step pattern, and a ROLL button that opens it across the whole window. See [EDITOR_ROLL.md](EDITOR_ROLL.md).

## MIDI
Part 1 listens on channel 1 and part 2 on channel 2 (editable). In the emulator, a WebMIDI keyboard or controller sends notes and controllers (the browser asks for MIDI access; clock is not passed on). On the device: clock in, program change and bank select, the mod wheel (CC1) moves the MORPH fader and is also a matrix source. CC 20 morph, 21 roll A, 22 roll B, 23 undo, 25 amount, 111 morph target, 112 mode. In LAYER, program change 0 to 15 on part 1's channel recalls MULTIs M01 to M16. Not yet tested with a range of real MIDI devices.
