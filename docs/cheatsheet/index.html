cheat sheet for the M-VAVE FM-1

checked against DUELL 1.0 · community-style quick reference; full details in SPEC.md and docs/QUICKSTART.md

Getting around

Turn SELECT
Previous / next sub-page; in lists and the menu it moves the row.
Tap HOME
MORPH page (HOME's round: MORPH, MORPH SET, MODE).
Hold HOME
Opens the menu. SELECT moves row to row; any knob or OCT+ / OCT- changes the value; HOME closes it.
Press ALGORITHM
Selects the part you edit (1 or 2). On the MORPH page and on PERFORM: PART 1, BOTH, PART 2.
Tap GLO, then turn SELECT
Steps through the GLO pages, first MIXER.
Hold EDIT / LFO / ENV / ARP / GLO
Quick edit: the four knobs become performance controls. The keys still play. Let go and you are back on your page.
Hold OCT- + turn a knob
Resets that knob's card to its default.
OCT- / OCT+ in a dialog or menu
OCT- means back / no, OCT+ means do it / yes.
MENU > SYSTEM > GUIDE
Shows the first-start guide again.

Playing

Tap OCT- / OCT+
Octave down / up of the keys. Both together reset the octave to 0.
Tap REC (not on sequencer pages)
HOLD switch for the part(s) the keys play; bright light = HOLD on. Tap again to release.
Tap PLAY/STOP
Stopped: starts when you let go. Running: stops at once.
Hold PLAY/STOP 0.6 s, or press it twice
STOP ALL: silences everything, delay, chorus and reverb tails too.
Hold GLO + keys / knobs
Keys play; knob 1 LEVEL 1, knob 2 PAN 1, knob 3 LEVEL 2, knob 4 PAN 2. OCT- mutes / unmutes part 1, OCT+ part 2.
ARP (tap / hold)
Arpeggiator of the selected part. Hold: QUICK ARP.
Mod wheel (CC1)
Moves the MORPH fader.

Sound

Tap HOME, turn knob 1
MORPH: glide from End A to End B (MIDI: CC70).
MORPH page, knob 2
ROLL: a new random end (turn left = A, right = B; MIDI: CC102, 0 to 42 rolls End A, 85 to 127 rolls End B).
MORPH page, knob 3
UNDO the last roll of A or B.
MORPH page, knob 4
AMOUNT: how far a roll may go (MIDI: CC103).
Turn BLEND on the TWIN FILTER
Reads MULTI, BOTH or TWIN.
Tap SCL
SCALE page (whole instrument). Second page CHORD (selected part).

Sequencer / patterns

Tap SEQ
Opens the 16-step pattern (32 patterns, P01 to P32) of the arp's SEQUENCER mode.
Tap REC on STEP / AUTOMATION or in the SEQ layer
Arms recording on the selected part. A red dot and REC show in the bottom row of every page.
Tap SAVE on STEP / GRID / AUTOMATION
Opens PATTERNS (32 patterns, P01 to P32).
Hold SAVE on PATTERNS
"SAVE OVER <name>?" for the slot picked; OCT+ says yes.

Effects

Tap FX (again)
The part's own pages. Tap again for the next sub-page.
SEND FX page, turn knob 1 / 2 / 3
Jumps to the CHORUS, DELAY 1/2 or REVERB page of GLO.
Hold FX (or hold FX + a white key)
PERFORM page. Tap FX to leave.
PERFORM: hold white keys
REPEAT 1/8, REPEAT 1/16, REPEAT 1/32, REVERSE; TAPE STOP, FREEZE, OCT UP, OCT DN; FLANGER, PHASER, DRIVE, PUMP; ROLL A, ROLL B, MUTE 1, MUTE 2.
GLO > COMPRESSOR, knob 1 RATIO
Starts at OFF. Turn up and THRESH, ATTACK, RELEASE appear.

Saving & loading

Tap SAVE
Opens the page that fits: PATTERNS on a sequencer page, otherwise MULTIS in LAYER and PATCHES in DUEL and STEREO. SELECT moves between PATCHES, PATTERNS, MULTIS, TOOLS.
Hold SAVE
Asks to overwrite the slot your sound came from. Knob 1 picks another slot, knob 2 RENAME, OCT+ yes, OCT- no.
Hold SAVE on MORPH page, TARGET BOTH
"OVERWRITE BOTH PATCHES?", each part over its own slot.
Hold SAVE on MULTIS
"SAVE OVER <name>?"; OCT+ yes. An empty multi slot asks for a name first.
SAVE > TOOLS, RESTORE ALL
Every patch slot gets its factory sound.
Backup (installer page or editor Backup page)
Saves patches, multis, patterns, user wavetables and settings. Back up before every flash.

Good to know

  • The PATCH LEVEL is saved with the patch; the PART LEVEL on the mixer is yours.
  • AFTERTOUCH is on by default; KEY VELOCITY default is OFF; switch it ON to play dynamically.
  • BASS MONO, WIDTH and COMPRESSOR belong to the unit, not to a patch or multi.
  • Teach a CC with MIDI LEARN on the unit. CC1 mod wheel, CC11 expression, CC64 sustain pedal are inputs.
