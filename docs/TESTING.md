# What to test: DUELL 1.0

Thank you for testing. You can test, at your own risk, on an FM-1 with DUELL 1.0 (see [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)). Web emulator: coming soon. It has been run on one real unit only, the author's, so reports from other units are especially welcome.

## What to test

- [ ] **DUEL / STEREO / LAYER**: DUEL keeps part 1 only on the left and part 2 only on the right (with effects on). STEREO is full stereo. LAYER plays both parts from the keys; try BALANCE, XFADE and the key SPLIT.
- [ ] **MORPH**: move the fader slowly A to B, ROLL A and B, UNDO, change AMOUNT. Any click, dropout, silence or painful sound? Does a patch always load at A?
- [ ] **Matrix**: assign a few of the 8 slots (LFO 2, ENV 2, velocity, aftertouch, mod wheel). Do ENV 2 and LFO 2 DEST pages do what they say?
- [ ] **MULTIs**: load the 16 factory multis (M01 to M16), then save, rename and erase your own. Power-cycle the unit and load again.
- [ ] **PATTERNS**: load a factory pattern, edit and save over a slot, erase it, use it as the SEQUENCER arp pattern.
- [ ] **PATCHES**: browse by TYPE, mark favourites, hold SAVE (OVERWRITE #NN: turn knob 1 to pick another slot, knob 2 to RENAME: the NAME screen, OCT+ back to the dialog, OCT+ saves under the new name), RESTORE the slot (knob 4), RESTORE ALL on SAVE > TOOLS, MIDI program change = slot. Fill all 128 slots by saving over each: there is no "full". Power-cycle the unit and check it all came back.
- [ ] **MIDI** (if your browser has WebMIDI, e.g. Chrome or Edge): part 1 on channel 1, part 2 on channel 2, no stuck notes; mod wheel (CC1) moves the morph; program change and bank select.
- [ ] **Sound palette**: audition the factory palette (about a hundred sounds, across the engines). Levels between sounds, anything harsh, stuck or silent?
- [ ] **Ducker / effects**: depth, attack, release; SHIM, RESO, PLATE (switch between all six reverb types while a tail rings: no click), ping-pong; no clicks.
- [ ] **Delay MODE** (GLO > DELAY 1/2, K1): DIGITAL (the old delay, PING off), PING-PONG (the old PING on), RANDOM and OCT UP. The pages: DELAY 1/2 MODE TIME FDBK COLR, DELAY 2/2 SPREAD MRATE MDEPTH MIX. DIGITAL does not show SPREAD (K1 stays empty), RANDOM / OCT UP do not show MRATE (K2 stays empty) and make K3 CHANCE. Turning MODE into RANDOM with CHANCE at 0: the header says CHANCE 40 % and K3 shows 40; back to DIGITAL or PING-PONG the MDEPTH is 0 again. The repeats do not ping-pong in RANDOM / OCT UP. Plucks and a beat with FDBK 60..90, CHANCE 25..100: do the events land on the beat (an edge per delay time), is the octave-up climb tamed by COLR, does turning MODE while the delay rings click (also DIGITAL <-> PING-PONG), does it sound better than the ping-pong (your ears), PLAY restarts the beat. In DUEL (0.2.22) MODE is shown and all four modes work, mono on each part's side: PING sounds like DIGI, RAND and OCT UP stay in the middle of the part's side (the other channel stays silent), SPREAD is not shown. Demo files: host renders by `tests/dlyev_render.c`.
- [ ] **Stability**: play for a while with an arp running and many effects on; report any freeze or screen glitch.

## How to report a bug

Open an issue with the [bug report form on GitHub](https://github.com/oscillr/DUELL/issues/new/choose). Choose where it happened and give the version shown in ABOUT, your browser and computer if it is the editor or installer, the steps, what you expected and what happened. Please do not paste private information. No warranty; DUELL is not affiliated with M-VAVE.

# For people with hardware

The checklist items below need a real FM-1 or real MIDI gear. Flash only after reading [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md) and [RECOVERY.md](RECOVERY.md); you install at your own risk.

Hardware checklist:

- [ ] **Install** (INSTALL.md): did it finish, did the unit restart, does ABOUT show DUELL?
- [ ] **MIDI channels**: part 1 on channel 1, part 2 on channel 2; no stuck notes.
- [ ] **MIDI program change and bank select**: bank 0 and bank 1; in LAYER, program 0 to 15 recalls MULTIs.
- [ ] **MIDI clock**: arp, LFO and delay follow an external clock; start/stop.
- [ ] **CLK AUTO** (GLO > TEMPO, KNOB 4): start and stop a DAW or a Circuit Tracks clock on USB, then on TRS: does DUELL switch (header CLOCK USB / TRS), follow, and go back to its own tempo about 1 s after the clock stops, without stopping or stumbling?
- [ ] **USB audio input** (0.2.14): on macOS 15 or older (and on Windows, Linux, iOS if you have them), with MENU > SYSTEM > USB SERIAL OFF, is DUELL listed as an audio input and does it record? MIDI still there? (The computer may set the unit up as a new device once.)
- [ ] **LEDs during a save** (0.2.14): save a patch while LEDs are lit: the keys and buttons should go dark for a moment, not flash bright.
- [ ] **Mod wheel** (CC1) moves the morph; CC 20 to 25 and 111 to 118 behave.
- [ ] **Sidechain / ducker**: sources PUMP, PART and MIDI channel/note; depth, attack, release; no clicks.
- [ ] **Stability**: play for a while with an arp running and many effects on; report any freeze, reboot or screen glitch.

## CPU test (M16 STRESS TEST in every build; MENU > SYSTEM > TEST in developer builds only)

**The release has no TEST screen** (ABOUT still shows the CPU line). Since 0.2.23 every build has the factory multi **M16 STRESS TEST** (below); the four TEST multis of earlier developer builds are gone from the slots (the host tests keep them as test scenes). The TEST screen is for developer builds, made with the test mode on (`DUELL_TEST_MODE=1`, off by default):

- Device: `DUELL_TEST_MODE=1 ./build.sh` in `felucca-base/` (it compiles with `-DDUELL_TEST_MODE=1`; the factory multis are the same sixteen as in the release).
- Emulator: `DUELL_TEST_MODE=1 .venv/bin/python gen_only.py`, then `DUELL_TEST_MODE=1 sh felucca-base/web/emu/build.sh`.
- Host tests: `./host_tests.sh` builds its test binaries with the test mode on (the `release` group builds with it off and checks that the TEST page is absent and M01 to M16 intact).
- Load bench: `./host_tests.sh bench` (on a Mac, about a second) prints what the processor pays, in host instructions per sample and in % (33 a sample = 1 %; the device read about 0.75 to 0.9 of it on the FM6 / PHASE scene this scale was calibrated on, but more on stages with 64-bit arithmetic: see "CPU on the unit" below): each engine's voice, each INSERT type, SAT, EQ, DUCK, each reverb type, delay mode and the chorus (STEREO and DUEL), SHIMMER's PITCH and MOD, COMP, WIDTH, BASS MONO, and a worst-case scene per mode (LAYER, STEREO, DUEL) with the estimated voices a part before 100 %. It fails only when a figure grows more than 10 % over `felucca-base/tests/bench_baseline.txt`; after a change that is meant to cost more, `cd felucca-base && BENCH_UPDATE=1 build/host/cpu_bench` rewrites the baseline (review the diff). The source is `felucca-base/tests/cpu_bench.c`.
- DX7 import tests use synthetic banks generated in the test code (`web/test_dx7_synth.mjs`); no real banks are in the repository. To also run your own banks, set `DX7_BANKS=<folder of .syx banks>` (`web/test_librarian.mjs` converts them too; `./host_tests.sh dx7compat` runs the local engine / reference comparison; without the variable it prints `skipped: no banks` and passes).

We will need to know how hard the audio works on real units (hardware only). Hold **HOME** for the menu, go to the SYSTEM tab (SELECT) and open **TEST** (K1 right or OCT+); OCT- goes back, HOME or any page button leaves the menu. It shows the loaded MULTI, the ENGINE and PATCH of part 1 and part 2, and the CPU meter:

- **AVERAGE 1 S**: how much of the time budget the audio used, averaged over the last second (the bar has a mark at 80 %).
- **PEAK HOLD**: the worst single block of the last seconds. Above 100 % a block was late.
- **MISSED**: audio blocks that were late since the last RESET. Any number above 0 may be heard as a click or dropout.
- The word: OK, NEAR THE LIMIT (80 % and up), AT THE LIMIT (95 % and up), BLOCKS MISSED JUST NOW (a miss in the last 5 s).

Knobs: **K1 MORPH** and **K2 ROLL** as on the MORPH page, **K3 LOAD** steps through your multis and loads the one it stops on at once, **K4 RESET** (turn it) clears the peak hold and the missed count and starts a fresh average.

**M16 STRESS TEST** is a test, not music: the two dearest sounds (#37 PAN WHISTLE on PHYS and #60 DIGITAL PAD on WAVE) with every stage on, spread over the two parts (both 24 dB multimode filters; on part 1 the FLANG insert and SAT, on part 2 the EQ, the PUMP ducker and a 1/16 arp with a short gate and release; the chorus, the RANDOM delay and the SHIM reverb with PITCH BOTH on both; since 0.2.25 BOTH is gone and the stored BOTH plays UP), LAYER, 137 BPM. It is the heaviest realistic scene, calibrated (0.2.24) so that one held key plays on the unit; it shows how many notes your unit takes. Load it (SAVE > MULTIS, or K3 on the TEST screen), turn the master COMP on if you want it in the test (COMP is a setting of the unit, not of a multi), then hold one key, then two, then three, each for about 30 seconds, and note where clicks or dropouts start (with the TEST screen: press RESET first, read AVERAGE, PEAK HOLD and MISSED). It can be overwritten (SAVE over it; ERASE brings it back).

What the host model expects for the 0.2.24 M16 (100 % = the whole time budget; `CPU_SCEN=S build/host/cpu_scenarios_test`): LAYER, MORPH fader at A: 1 key 62 %, 2 keys 78 %, 3 keys and more 95 % (the voice cap holds it at 4 voices). With COMP on about +1.5 %. STEREO is the same as LAYER when part 2 gets the same notes on MIDI channel 2. DUEL: 1 key 74 %, 2 keys 90 %, 3 keys 107 %. 0.2.25 (SHIM UP instead of BOTH, the EQ's HIGH band now running, the EQ in 32-bit math): LAYER 63 / 80 / 97 %, DUEL 72 / 88 / 105 %; with the overload guard engaged (below) 58 / 74 / 91 % and 64 / 80 / 97 % (`CPU_SCEN=V`). (0.2.23's M16 was 78 / 110 / 136 % in LAYER and overloaded on the unit with one key, see "CPU on the unit".) What to read on the unit: AVERAGE and PEAK HOLD with 1, 2 and 3 keys, and MISSED. Report the pairs (model %, unit %): that is the missing calibration of the model for this scene.

**How to report**: the firmware version, your unit (and whether it is dual core off, the default), the final AVERAGE, PEAK HOLD, MISSED and the state word, whether you heard clicks or dropouts. A photo of the TEST page works. Please also report if the page itself seems to make things worse (the meter only reads while the page is open).

Do not turn on dual core if you see the option: it is off by default and untested.

## CPU on the unit

**How to read it.** The small bar under the battery (MENU > DISPLAY > CPU METER) is the audio's share of its time budget over the last second, in 8 steps: theme colour below 70 %, amber from 70 %, red from 85 %; the whole bar red for a second means a block was late (a click or a dropout is possible). ABOUT shows the same figure as a number; developer builds have the TEST screen above (AVERAGE, PEAK HOLD, MISSED). What you hear first is not the click: from 85 % on (two blocks in a row) the engine starts cutting voices, the released ones first (a pad's tail or an arp's last note ends early), so "the tails are chopped" is the first sign of overload; above 100 % blocks are late and click. The bar is measured on the unit (the audio interrupt's own time, the key scan taken out); the emulator only estimates it from the voice count.

**The model and the unit.** The host figures in this file and in the bench (`33 host instructions a sample = 1 %`) are instruction counts on a Mac. One scene was measured on the author's unit when the scale was set (a FM6 + PHASE test multi: the unit read about 0.75 to 0.9 of the model), and that ratio was written everywhere. It does not hold for every scene. What the device build's code shows (the pi32v2 disassembly of 0.2.23, `tools/build.py`'s objdump): the chip has no 64-bit multiply or 64-bit compare, so a stage written in 64-bit arithmetic costs about three times its host count: the part EQ's filter runs 85 device instructions a sample a band against about 26 on the host (the EQ's 2.4 % a part in the model is roughly 7 % on the unit; 0.2.25: rewritten in 32-bit multiplies, about 32 device instructions a band against about 28 on the host, and a flat band is not run at all); the multimode filter's 24 dB loop 153 against about 118 (1.3 x); the SHIM pitch shifter 186 a sample in BOTH (removed in 0.2.25) and the RANDOM delay's reads about 95 a voice (both about 1.5 x). On top of that the hot functions run from RAM but the rest of the audio code (PHYS, the RANDOM delay, SHIM's pitch shifter, the master stages, the mixer itself) runs from the SPI flash through a 32 KB instruction cache, and the 2026-10-15 bench on the unit showed that moving code between the two changes the load by up to 15 points either way (BASE 75 % to 89.5 % with more code in RAM: layout, not instruction count). So: the model's ranking of stages is good, its absolute figure for a scene heavy in EQ, PHYS, SHIM BOTH or RANDOM delay is too low. For 0.2.23's M16 the static count above puts the unit near 95 % at one key where the model said 78 %, which is what the author saw (overload at one note; not a measurement, an estimate). The only cure is a reading on the unit: the TEST screen, or the bar.

**Where 0.2.23's M16 cost went** (host model, LAYER, 1 key = 78 %, each stage switched off in turn; `CPU_SCEN=S` plus a scratch harness): the sends 21 % (the buses run whether fed or not; SHIM BOTH 3.8 more than the plain shimmer, the RANDOM delay 5.6 more than DIGITAL), the part stages 14.6 % (insert 5.1, EQ 5.2, SAT 3.0, ducker 1.4), the 24 dB slope 4.2 (against 12 dB), part 2's arp tails (REL 40 instead of the pad's 1.5 s: 6.5 % at one key and 22 % at two, the arp's overlapping voices), idle mix 15 %. 0.2.24's M16 keeps every stage on but spreads them over the two parts and cuts the arp voices overlapping: 62 % at one key.

**Safe polyphony, as the host model has it** (the unit: read the bar). The 8-voice pool is shared by both parts; the VOICE CAP (voice.c) limits each part so the predicted worst case stays under 85 % of the model, using per-engine costs (PHYS 14 %, FM6 6 %, VOICE 6 %, WHEEL 6 %, WAVE 5 %, ANALOG 5 %, NOISE 5 %, TRIO 4 %, PHASE 4 %, LOFI 2 %, + 2 % or 4 % for the 12 / 24 dB filter), without the effects. Factory multis M01 to M15 play 4 + 4 notes under 100 % of the model with the fader in the middle (`./host_tests.sh cpuscen`). The bench's worst case (both parts on the dearest PHYS sound, PHASR + SAT + EQ + DUCK on both, every send at 100, RANDOM delay, SHIM UP with MOD 50, COMP + WIDTH) fits about 2.1 voices a part in LAYER and STEREO and 1.8 in DUEL (0.2.25). In DUEL every bus runs twice (one per side), so the same two parts cost about 12 % more than in STEREO or LAYER; the master stages (COMP, WIDTH, SATURATOR, BASS MONO) do not run in DUEL. Rule of thumb for a multi of your own on the unit: keep the bar out of the red with the most keys you will hold; if it is red, the first things to turn off, by what they cost, are the RANDOM / OCT UP delay mode (PING-PONG instead, about 4 to 6 %), a part EQ you do not need (about 1 % a band in the model and, since 0.2.25, about the same on the unit; a band at 0 dB costs nothing), an insert (3 to 5 %), SAT (3 %) and the 24 dB slope (12 dB saves about 2 % a voice).

**The overload guard (0.2.25).** Near the limit the unit now lightens the dearest effect by itself: when two audio halves in a row take more than 85 % of their time (the same rule that starts cutting voices), the delay's RANDOM or OCT UP mode plays as the plain delay, faded over about 12 ms; when the CPU bar's figure has stayed under 70 % (out of the amber) for about 3 s, the events fade back in. Between 70 and 85 % it stays as it is. Nothing is stored and nothing changes below the threshold. **How to see it:** ABOUT shows **LIGHT FX** at the right of the USB line while it is engaged (developer builds: the console's status prints `fx_light` and `fx_light_count`). In the host model it takes about 5.5 points off M16 in LAYER and 8.8 in DUEL; on the unit probably more (the event delay's reads cost about 1.5 times their host count there). **What to report:** with M16 and two or three keys, whether LIGHT FX comes and goes (and how often), whether you hear the delay change character when it does, and whether clicks or dropouts start later than before. If it keeps switching every few seconds at one load, say so: the 3 s hold may need to be longer.
