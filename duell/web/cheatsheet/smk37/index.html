SMK-37 Cheat Sheet

DUELL 1.3.6 · FM-1 Cheat Sheet

Two parts

Knobs 1 to 4
Part 1, on every page.
Knobs 5 to 8
Part 2, on every page. On a page for the whole unit they do what knobs 1 to 4 do.
Three colours
Part 1 and part 2 each have their colour; a third colour marks what sets the whole unit.

Switch parts

GLOBE + CHN 1 (C3)
Part 1 only.
GLOBE + CHN 2 (C#3)
Part 2 only.
GLOBE + CHN 1 CHN 2
Both parts.
Key split
In STEREO and DUEL part 1 plays the low keys and part 2 the high keys, from F#4. Move it: BT MENU > PLAYING > SPLIT.

Faders

FADER 1 / FADER 2
LEVEL of part 1 / part 2.
FADER 3 / FADER 4
MORPH of part 1 / part 2.

Buttons

< / >
The pages of a module.
PATCH
MORPH, MACROS 1/2 and 2/2, PATCHES, PATTERNS, MULTIS, TOOLS, in turn.
KNOB BANK
LFO.
FADER BANK
ENVELOPE.
KNOB BANK + FADER BANK
MOD MATRIX.
PARA / SCALE / FX / GLOBE / ARP / SEQ
EDIT, SCALE, FX, the global pages, ARP and the sequencer.
BT
MENU.
SEQ PLAY / SEQ REC
PLAY/STOP and REC, as on the FM-1: SEQ REC is HOLD, and records on the sequencer pages. SEQ PLAY twice: STOP ALL.

Pads and PERFORM

Hold a pad
An effect. Press harder for more of it.
NOTE REPEAT
PERFORM: your eight knobs.
Hold NOTE REPEAT + turn a knob
On a control's page: puts that control on PERFORM.

Quick edits

Hold PARA / SCALE / GLOBE / ARP / SEQ / SEQ REC
The main knobs over the page you are on. Let go to go back.
ARP + 1/4 .. 1/32T
ARP RATE, from the printed keys.
GLOBE + C .. B
SCALE ROOT, from the printed keys.
Hold OCT- + turn a knob
Resets that knob to its default.
Hold a step's key + OCT+ + turn a knob
PARAMETER LOCK: the step's own value.

Install

Installer
Picks the package for your model.
