# BENCH-HEAVY (2026-11-04), a bench suite for the voice share (VS_SHARE): core 1 renders half of part 1's voices while part 2 is silent.
# COPY of DUELL/working docs/patches/bench-heavy-scenes_2026-11-04.txt (the source of record: edit THAT file, then copy it here again). The sh_* counters are in the result line since the 2026-11-04 wiring (bench.c, bench_run.py SH_COLS).
# Needs: the 12 patches of bench-heavy-patches_2026-11-04.json in slots #117..#128 and (for the last group) the 4 multis of bench-heavy-multis_2026-11-04.json in M13..M16, on FM1_901 only, a bench build (BENCHV7 or newer, TC_MEAS).
# Run: python3 tools/bench_run.py --onset --suite BENCH-HEAVY            (--only 'BH_S1_*' for one group)
# Estimated time, one run, no repeats: about 8 minutes (43 tests of about 10 s: 6 s of play, 1 s tail, setup). Estimate, not measured.
# Scene: STEREO (each part its own channel), only channel 1 plays, so part 2 (BENCH LOFI, loaded, not muted) is silent and the share can run. Chord keys DIA7 OPEN are in each patch: 1 key = 4 voices.
#   S1  2 keys = 8 voices, budget ON (the number the owner sees), two cores   -> the share runs; read notes1 (AVAILABLE VOICES) against vox
#   S2  3 keys = 12 voices, budget OPEN (past every cap), two cores           -> the share under overload
#   S3  as S1 with core 1 treated as down (tune core1 off)                  -> the one-core reference of S1 (the share must not run: sh_posts 0)
#   S4  both parts play, 2 keys each, budget ON                              -> the normal two-core case (the share must not run: sh_posts 0)
#   S5  the four multis from the multi file, same keys as S1 / S4
# Globals (same in every test; a multi holds the first four, MASTER is the device's): delay OCT+ (G_DMODE 15 = 3), reverb SHIM (G_RTYPE 24 = 3) pitch DOWN (G_SHPITCH 26 = 1) mod 50 (G_SHMOD 17 = 50), MASTER COMP 4, WIDTH 150, BASS MONO 3.
# Pass (as POLY-MAX-BUDGET-A, item 139): 0 silent, 0 pauses, 0 missed halves over the whole run, shed 0, LIGHT FX 0, peak <= 90 %.
